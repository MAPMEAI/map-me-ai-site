Nội dung prompt

First Frame Image Prompt:
A vertical cinematic shot of a man in a traditional navy blue martial arts uniform with bright yellow shoes, hair tied in a topknot, balanced in a deep squat on a single horizontal bamboo pole. His arms are extended forward in a focused martial arts pose. The bamboo pole spans a narrow, deep gorge between two sheer, dark gray rock cliff faces. Several other bamboo poles are visible above, forming a ladder-like structure ascending the chasm. In the background, misty green mountains fade into a hazy, overcast sky. The atmosphere is tense, awe-inspiring, and epic. Cool natural lighting, earthy color palette with dark gray rocks, pale yellow bamboo, navy blue clothing, and misty green mountains. Photorealistic, cinematic, high detail.

Image-to-Video Prompt:
Theme: A martial artist performs a death-defying acrobatic ascent across bamboo poles spanning a deep mountain gorge.
Visuals: A deep rocky canyon with sheer dark gray cliff faces, a series of thin horizontal bamboo poles bridging the chasm, misty green mountains in the far distance, soft overcast natural light. The man wears a navy blue traditional martial arts uniform and bright yellow shoes, hair in a topknot.
Camera: Static wide side-angle positioned on the cliff face, slowly tilting upward to track the subject’s vertical ascent, keeping the dizzying drop visible below [0-30s].
Style: Cinematic, photorealistic, epic wuxia atmosphere, cool-toned, high-stakes, graceful and dangerous.
Action + Sound Design:
[0-3s] The man holds a deep, perfectly balanced squat on the lowest bamboo pole, arms extended forward, eyes locked ahead — ambient mountain wind, distant mist, tense silence.
[3-6s] He dips slightly, then explodes upward into a powerful backflip, rotating cleanly in mid-air before landing softly on the next bamboo pole above — rush of air, light bamboo flex, controlled landing thud.
[6-9s] Without pausing, he springs into a twisting front flip, body spinning horizontally as he crosses the gap, landing in another low squat on the next pole — bamboo creak, fabric whip, sharp exhale.
[9-12s] He continues upward with rapid successive jumps: a sideways aerial twist, arms tucked, then extended for balance, sticking each landing with martial precision — rhythmic impacts, wind rushing past the gorge walls.
[12-15s] He executes a running leap across a wider gap, briefly catching the next pole with one hand, swinging his legs up and over in one fluid motion to regain his footing — hand slap on bamboo, body torque, gravel dust.
[15-18s] He pauses in a low squat to center himself, chest rising and falling, scanning the remaining poles above — heavy controlled breathing, wind swirling through the chasm.
[18-21s] He launches again, performing a high back layout with a full twist, silhouetted against the misty mountains before landing lightly on a higher pole — long air time, bamboo vibration, soft impact.
[21-24s] He bounds upward once more with a quick front somersault, catching the final bamboo pole with both hands, body swinging beneath it before he pulls himself up into a crouch — hand grip strain, pole flex, momentum shift.
[24-27s] He readies himself on the final pole, coiling low, then pushes off toward a wooden platform built into the cliffside beside a traditional Chinese pavilion — focused grunt, wind surge.
[27-30s] He completes a final dramatic flip through the air and lands cleanly on the platform, rising smoothly into a formal martial arts stance, arms at his sides, breathing heavily as the camera settles — solid landing thud, deep exhales, wind fading into silence.
