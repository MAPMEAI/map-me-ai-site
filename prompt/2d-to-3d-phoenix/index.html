2D to 3D Phoenix

Bức vẽ phượng hoàng 2D trên trang vở biến thành thực thể 3D sống động.

Miễn phíVideoVideo

Nội dung prompt

1. First Frame Image Prompt:
An open notebook resting on a wooden desk, featuring a detailed, childlike colored-pencil illustration of a phoenix taking up most of the visible page. The drawing is rich and intricate — vibrant orange, golden, and blue pencil strokes forming the wings, head, body, small claws, and long, elegantly curling tail feathers that sweep across the paper.
Key Details:
- Setting: Open notebook on a wooden desk or table, warm natural window light coming from one side, casting a soft golden glow across the page.
- Phoenix Illustration: Completely flat, two-dimensional, and fully intact colored-pencil artwork — textured paper visible beneath the strokes, warm orange and amber tones on the body, golden highlights on the wings, delicate blue decorative details near the tail feathers.
- Paper Texture: Slightly worn notebook page with visible grain, faint pencil outline sketches barely peeking beneath the colored strokes, giving it an authentic hand-drawn look.
- Atmosphere: Calm, warm, and quiet — no movement, no glow, no sparks — just the stillness of a beautifully drawn illustration waiting to come alive.
- Composition: Camera angled slightly from above looking down at the notebook, phoenix centered on the page with wings spread and tail feathers curling gracefully toward the bottom of the frame, plenty of empty page space around it to allow room for the coming lift and transformation.
- Style: Photorealistic rendering with fine art illustration preserved exactly as a child’s colored-pencil drawing — sharp focus on the notebook and phoenix artwork, cinematic soft natural lighting, shallow depth of field.

2. Image-to-Video Prompt:
Create one continuous 8-second transformation. A realistic hand enters from the upper right and gently pinches the upper tip of one wing of the drawn phoenix on the notebook page. The hand slowly lifts the entire illustrated phoenix away from the paper. At first, the complete bird separates from the page as one thin, flexible childlike colored-pencil sticker, including both wings, head, body, claws and every long curling tail feather. The flat drawing bends and flexes naturally as it is peeled away, preserving exactly the orange, golden and blue colored-pencil design.
While the same flat phoenix is being lifted above the notebook, it gradually gains depth and physical volume. The colored-pencil strokes continuously develop into thousands of layered orange, amber and golden feathers. The wings become dimensional and luminous, the body gains realistic feather structure, the long illustrated tail feathers extend into flowing three-dimensional plumage while preserving their original curves and the small blue decorative feather details. The transformation happens continuously on the same object with no sudden morph, replacement or second bird.
A soft warm glow gradually appears inside the feathers. Tiny golden sparks and a few glowing feather particles begin drifting around the phoenix without obscuring it. The hand releases the fully formed bird and completely exits the frame. The phoenix gently lifts higher above the notebook, slowly moving its wings and tail feathers in the warm air. Dimensional clouds gradually form around it while strong golden sunlight enters through the window, creating delicate volumetric rays and glowing feather edges. The camera smoothly follows the phoenix slightly upward and backward, ending with the majestic luminous bird hovering above the open notebook.
No cuts, no extra hands, no duplicated phoenix, no sudden explosion, no flames covering the bird, no abrupt transformation, no text. Natural sounds of paper peeling, feathers unfolding, soft wing movement, subtle glowing crackles, drifting sparks and light breeze, no music.
