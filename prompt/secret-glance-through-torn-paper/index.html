Secret Glance Through Torn Paper

Nhân vật hé mắt qua lớp giấy rách, cận cảnh đôi mắt cuốn hút.

Nội dung prompt

A character peeking through a torn-paper effect. A sheet of white paper is ripped horizontally across the center, creating a narrow opening that reveals the character's eyes. One delicate hand emerges slightly from behind the paper, resting lightly on the torn edge.
Extreme close-up focused on the eyes and fingers. Deep, captivating pupils convey a dreamy, languid, mysterious gaze with a subtle hint of flirtation. Thick, individually defined eyelashes and refined eye makeup enhance the character's expressive features. Slender, elegant fingers with neatly trimmed nails gently touch the torn paper.
Semi-thick-painting illustration style, soft and natural skin coloring with realistic dimensionality, smooth shadow transitions, and delicate skin texture without heavy brushstrokes. Harmonious colors create a dreamy, romantic, slightly ambiguous atmosphere.
Soft directional lighting from the upper left, glossy highlights in the eyes, and finely rendered shadows along the torn paper edges enhance depth and realism. The intimate peek-through composition feels secretive and alluring.
Emphasize the eyes and fingertips while keeping the hand subtly understated. Preserve generous areas of blank white paper around the narrow opening. Refined composition, delicate details, soft luminous lighting, elegant visual storytelling.
