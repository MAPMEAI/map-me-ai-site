Combo 2 Video Tiệm Bánh Trong Thân Cây Cổ Thụ — Đội Thợ Tí Hon Xây Tiệm Bánh (Part 1 + Part 2)

Combo 2 prompt video nối tiếp (mỗi phần 10s dọc 9:16, Flow): đội thợ tí hon xây tiệm bánh trong thân cây cổ thụ (Part 1) và tiệm bánh hoàn thiện đón khách (Part 2) — khóa liên tục khung hình giữa 2 phần.

Nội dung prompt

PART 1 — BUILDING THE MAGICAL TREE BAKERY

Google Flow | Exactly 10 Seconds | Vertical 9:16 | 60 FPS

VISUAL STYLE:
Ultra-photorealistic miniature-world cinematography, premium handcrafted diorama aesthetic, macro photography, extraordinary environmental details, realistic miniature human characters, warm cinematic sunlight, physically believable construction activities and natural depth of field.

MAIN CONCEPT:
A team of tiny professional craftsmen transforms the enormous hollow trunk of an ancient forest tree into a beautiful luxury woodland bakery.

ENVIRONMENT:
A magical yet realistic green forest with enormous moss-covered tree roots, tiny wildflowers, mushrooms, miniature wooden cottages, natural stones and warm sunlight filtering through the leaves.

At the center stands a massive hollow tree trunk with enough interior space for a tiny bakery.

WORKERS:
Exactly FIVE tiny human craftsmen, each approximately 5–7 cm tall, wearing miniature brown work trousers, cream shirts, green work aprons and small protective construction helmets.

Maintain identical worker appearances and clothing throughout the video.

CAMERA:
Premium macro cinematography with realistic miniature scale. Start with a wide establishing shot, then use smooth close-up tracking movements to capture the construction process.

ACTION — EXACT TIMING:

[0.0–2.5s] — THE EMPTY TREE

A beautiful miniature forest village appears in a cinematic wide shot.

The camera slowly pushes toward an enormous hollow tree trunk.

Five tiny craftsmen are already working around the entrance. One measures the wooden opening, two carry miniature wooden planks, and another operates a tiny electric cutting tool.

Small wood particles fall naturally onto the moss-covered ground.

[2.5–5.0s] — BUILDING THE ENTRANCE

Close-up construction sequence.

Two workers carefully install a beautiful curved wooden entrance frame inside the hollow tree.

Another worker climbs a miniature ladder and secures a small circular window frame.

A fourth worker carries a tiny wooden door while the fifth measures the floor.

All movements must look physically realistic, with visible tool contact and believable object weight.

[5.0–7.5s] — THE GLASS WINDOWS

The craftsmen carefully install curved transparent glass panels around the bakery entrance.

One worker holds a glass panel while another secures its wooden frame.

Warm sunlight reflects naturally across the transparent glass.

A tiny worker wipes away dust using a miniature cloth.

[7.5–10.0s] — THE BAKERY TAKES SHAPE

The camera slowly pulls back to reveal the partially completed tree bakery.

The curved glass entrance, wooden doorway, tiny roof details and miniature wooden steps are now installed.

One worker carries a miniature wooden bakery sign toward the entrance while the others continue finishing the structure.

END FRAME:
A beautiful partially constructed woodland bakery inside the giant hollow tree. Workers are still actively building it.

PART 2 must continue from this exact unfinished bakery structure.

SOUND DESIGN:
Natural forest ambience, tiny hammer taps, realistic miniature wood cutting, screwdriver sounds, ladder movements, glass placement, footsteps on wooden surfaces, birds and soft woodland wind.

CONTINUITY LOCK:
Same five workers, identical clothing, same giant hollow tree, same forest, same entrance architecture, same glass panels, same camera scale and consistent warm sunlight.

NEGATIVE INSTRUCTIONS:
No instant construction, no magical appearance of completed structures, no extra workers, no disappearing characters, no oversized human characters, no cartoon rendering, no plastic toy appearance, no floating tools, no impossible physics, no changing tree shape, no flickering, no text, no subtitles, no watermark or logos.

OUTPUT:
Exactly 10 seconds, vertical 9:16, ultra-photorealistic premium miniature-world filmmaking.

==========

PART 2 — THE BAKERY COMES ALIVE

Google Flow | Exactly 10 Seconds | Vertical 9:16 | 60 FPS

REFERENCE & CONTINUITY:
Continue directly from PART 1.

Use the exact final frame of Part 1 as the opening reference.

Preserve the same five tiny craftsmen, their faces, clothing, proportions, the enormous hollow tree, wooden entrance, curved glass windows, forest surroundings and warm lighting.

VISUAL STYLE:
Ultra-photorealistic miniature-world filmmaking, premium macro commercial cinematography, detailed food textures, realistic miniature human movements, warm golden lighting and exceptionally detailed handcrafted environments.

MAIN CONCEPT:
The tiny craftsmen complete the woodland bakery and transform it into a fully functioning miniature artisan bakery.

ACTION — EXACT TIMING:

[0.0–2.0s] — COMPLETING THE INTERIOR

The camera moves smoothly through the partially completed wooden entrance.

The five craftsmen finish installing the interior wooden counter, miniature display shelves, warm hanging lanterns and a beautiful brick bread oven.

One worker carries a tiny wooden chair while another carefully places a small round table near the window.

The bakery interior gradually becomes a cozy, professionally designed space.

[2.0–4.0s] — THE BAKERS ARRIVE

A seamless close-up reveals three tiny professional bakers wearing cream chef uniforms, white miniature chef hats and brown aprons.

One baker kneads fresh dough on a wooden counter.

Another carefully shapes small croissants while the third places prepared bread into a miniature brick oven.

Visible flour particles float naturally through the warm sunlight.

[4.0–6.0s] — FRESH BREAD PREPARATION

Extreme macro food shot.

The tiny baker opens the miniature oven and carefully removes a tray of freshly baked golden croissants and artisan bread.

Realistic steam rises from the freshly baked products.

Another baker arranges golden pastries inside a beautiful glass display cabinet.

A tiny worker places fresh bread into a wicker basket.

The food must look genuinely appetizing, with crisp crusts, realistic textures and golden-brown surfaces.

[6.0–8.0s] — THE FIRST CUSTOMERS

The camera moves outside through the curved glass entrance.

Two tiny customers arrive wearing miniature casual woodland clothing.

A tiny baker welcomes them and places a small basket of freshly baked pastries on the wooden counter.

Warm golden light illuminates the finished bakery interior.

A small outdoor seating area is visible beneath the enormous tree roots.

[8.0–10.0s] — THE GRAND REVEAL

The camera slowly pulls backward and rises slightly to reveal the completed woodland bakery.

The entire hollow tree has become an extraordinary miniature artisan bakery.

Its beautiful curved glass entrance glows with warm golden light.

Tiny customers sit at wooden tables, bakers prepare fresh pastries, and the five original craftsmen admire their completed creation.

Tiny lanterns illuminate the surrounding mushrooms, moss-covered roots and woodland pathways.

FINAL FRAME:
A breathtaking wide macro cinematic view of the fully operational tiny forest bakery, surrounded by an enchanted green woodland.

SOUND DESIGN:
Tiny footsteps, dough kneading, flour brushing, oven door opening, realistic bread tray sounds, gentle bakery ambience, soft customer chatter, birdsong and forest wind.

CAMERA:
Smooth macro tracking, detailed food close-ups and a slow cinematic final pullback. Maintain consistent miniature scale and natural depth of field.

CONTINUITY LOCK:
Same hollow tree, same five construction workers, same bakery architecture, same glass windows, same wooden counter, same forest and identical lighting from Part 1.

NEGATIVE INSTRUCTIONS:
No instant transformations, no disappearing workers, no changing character appearances, no oversized food, no floating pastries, no unrealistic baking physics, no cartoon appearance, no plastic-looking textures, no camera jumps, no distorted miniature people, no text, no subtitles, no watermark or logos.

OUTPUT:
Exactly 10 seconds, vertical 9:16, premium photorealistic miniature-world cinematic quality.
