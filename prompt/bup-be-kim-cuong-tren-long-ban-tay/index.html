Nội dung prompt

10s vertical video]

FORMAT: 9:16 vertical, 10 seconds, macro/close-up photoreal video.

SUBJECT: A miniature fashion-doll figurine (~12cm tall) with ADULT proportions — classic fashion-mannequin face (defined cheekbones, mature facial structure, normal adult eye-to-face ratio, NOT large chibi eyes or oversized head), styled hair with fine diamond strands woven through it. Entire outfit and accessories are covered in realistic cut diamonds — fitted top, tailored shorts or trousers, choker, hoop earrings, mini handbag — each diamond catching and refracting light individually, sharp prismatic sparkle (hard diamond fire/refraction, not soft glitter).

SCENE: The doll stands on the open palm of a real adult human hand (natural skin texture, French-tip manicured nails). A second hand enters mid-clip to adjust an accessory. Background is a blurred studio bokeh with soft circular light reflections.

CAMERA: Macro lens, shallow depth of field, mostly static with subtle handheld micro-motion; push-in during the final reveal beat.

ACTION TIMELINE (10s, with viral hook ending):
0-2s: Palm opens, revealing the diamond doll standing, casual light sway, hand tilts slightly so diamonds catch the light across the outfit.
2-4s: Second hand enters, lifts the doll's hair to show the diamond choker and earrings sparkling.
4-6s: Close-up on the doll's diamond-covered top — light sweeps across, causing a rapid flash/glint transition (diamond fire effect).
6-8s: Second hand places a small diamond-studded handbag on the doll's wrist — doll's expression subtly shifts to a confident smirk/pose (idle animation).
8-10s (HOOK/TWIST): Sudden fast push-in on the doll's face as it "looks" directly into camera with a confident smirk, timed with a sharp diamond flash/light burst across frame — freeze-like beat on the last frame for a scroll-stopping finish.

LIGHTING: Bright, high-key studio lighting with hard specular highlights to maximize diamond refraction; warm neutral skin tone on the hand.

STYLE: Photoreal macro photography blended with stylized 3D fashion-doll rendering (adult mannequin face, NOT childlike proportions) — "toy comes to life" hybrid look, ultra-detailed, high-gloss, crisp diamond sparkle.

MOOD/AUDIO CUE: Upbeat, glam, confident — sync the final smirk/flash beat to a musical hit/drop if audio is added in post.

NEGATIVE PROMPT: no childlike facial proportions, no oversized eyes/head, no extra/deformed fingers, no blurry face, no melting or smeared diamonds, no jittery unnatural motion, no text/ưatermarke, no plastic/rhinestone look (must read as real cut diamond, not glitter).
