Same Girl Different Decades

Cùng 1 cô gái qua 6 thập kỷ thời trang 1950s–Y2K

Miễn phíẢnhFashion

Nội dung prompt

Create a photo collage of the same girl (face based on the uploaded photo, keep 100% facial features) styled across 6 different decades: 1950s (polka dot dress, pearl necklace, gloves), 1960s (mod shift dress, headband, vintage camera), 1970s (bohemian paisley, headscarf, round sunglasses), 1980s (neon windbreaker, scrunchie, boombox), 1990s (oversized sweater, jeans, round glasses), Y2K (pink metallic top, butterfly clips, tinted sunglasses). Each era with authentic background props. Text overlay: 'SAME GIRL DIFFERENT DECADES'.
