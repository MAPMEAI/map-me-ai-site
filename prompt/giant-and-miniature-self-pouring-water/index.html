Giant and Miniature Self Pouring Water

Ảnh fantasy 2 phiên bản cùng người rót nước vào ly khổng lồ, 4:5.

Miễn phíẢnhẢnh

Nội dung prompt

Use my uploaded photo as the main identity reference. Create an ultra-realistic, high-quality, full-body fantasy photograph featuring TWO versions of me in the same scene.

MAIN SCENE:

A giant version of me stands on a rustic wooden platform in a beautiful, lush green forest. I am wearing a black polo shirt, black cargo trousers, clean white sneakers, dark sunglasses, and a black wristwatch. I have a natural, confident facial expression and realistic body proportions.

The giant version of me holds a shiny stainless-steel bucket with both hands, carefully pouring a stream of crystal-clear water into an enormous transparent drinking glass positioned on a large tree stump.

MINIATURE VERSION OF ME:

Inside the giant glass, create a tiny, realistic version of me wearing exactly the same black outfit, white sneakers, sunglasses, and wristwatch. The miniature version stands inside the glass with both hands raised above my head as water splashes around me. Make the miniature person clearly recognizable as the same person as the giant version.

WATER EFFECTS:

Show realistic, crystal-clear water flowing from the metal bucket into the oversized glass. Capture dramatic water splashes, suspended droplets, ripples, reflections, and bubbles. The miniature person must be clearly visible through the transparent glass.

BACKGROUND AND LIGHTING:

A beautiful, dense tropical forest with lush green foliage, natural sunlight filtering through the trees, soft cinematic lighting, realistic shadows, and a slightly blurred background. Include a weathered wooden walkway and a large tree stump supporting the glass.

CAMERA AND QUALITY:

Vertical 4:5 composition, full-body framing, professional photography, sharp focus, realistic skin texture, accurate hands, detailed clothing, physically believable water effects, cinematic depth of field, HDR, 8K-quality detail.

IDENTITY PRESERVATION:

Use my uploaded photo to preserve my actual facial features, natural skin tone, hairstyle, and identity. Do not substitute another person's face or apply artificial beauty filters.

IMPORTANT:

Both versions must look like the same person. The giant person must be outside the glass, pouring water into it, while the miniature version stands inside the glass with both hands raised. Keep the entire bucket, water stream, glass, and both versions of me visible in the frame.

NEGATIVE PROMPT:

Blurry image, cartoon, anime, distorted face, incorrect identity, extra fingers, missing limbs, duplicated body parts, deformed hands, floating bucket, broken glass, opaque glass, unnatural water, unrealistic proportions, cropped feet, cropped head, text, logo, watermark.
