Triple-Exposure Fashion — 'I Don't Fit Inside The Frame' (3 Lớp, Bước Ra Khỏi Khung Kính)

Prompt ảnh dọc 4:5 triple-exposure thời trang: cùng 1 người ở 3 lớp không gian — chân dung khổ lớn nền sau, bản thứ hai bước xuyên qua khung kính treo, bản thứ ba sải bước tiền cảnh — studio sang trọng, ánh sáng ngày ấm.

Miễn phíẢnhPortrait

Nội dung prompt

Create a breathtaking ultra-realistic cinematic TRIPLE-EXPOSURE fashion artwork using my uploaded face as the strict identity reference.
**MAIN CONCEPT:** "I DON'T FIT INSIDE THE FRAME."
A photograph that looks physically impossible, but every individual element must appear to have been photographed in a real luxury studio.
**COMPOSITION:** Vertical 4:5, three versions of the SAME recognisable man interacting across three different spatial layers.
**LAYER 1, BACKGROUND:** A gigantic elegant close-up of my face spanning the entire upper background. My head is turned dramatically towards the left, never directly facing the camera. I wear sophisticated gold-framed sunglasses with warm brown lenses, a cream leather jacket and a sharp modern undercut with textured top. The enormous portrait resembles a luxurious printed fashion photograph, slightly desaturated and seamlessly blended with the architecture.
**LAYER 2, MIDDLE:** A second version of me is literally stepping through a huge suspended rectangular glass picture frame. My upper body is still inside the image, but my right leg, one hand and part of my jacket have crossed into the real foreground space. The glass frame has realistic thickness, reflections, cast shadows, physical edges and perspective.
I wear a deep burgundy suede biker jacket over an ivory open-collar shirt, tailored stone trousers and polished cream leather shoes. Hairstyle: relaxed swept-back waves with tapered sides. I am looking down naturally while stepping forward.
**LAYER 3, FOREGROUND:** A third full-body version of me strides diagonally across the foreground while casually adjusting stylish oval sunglasses. I wear a cobalt-blue luxury leather jacket, soft cream knitwear, tailored beige trousers and clean white sneakers. Hairstyle: modern textured crop with volume. One shoe extends slightly towards the camera, creating dramatic but physically plausible perspective.
**SURROUNDINGS:** A bright editorial studio with sophisticated ivory walls, enormous curved red sculptural panels, soft glossy flooring, elegant natural shadows and scattered fragments of reflective transparent glass.
One frame appears to cast a shadow onto another, creating the illusion that all three scenes exist in the same physical room.
**LIGHTING:** Beautiful warm commercial daylight from high studio windows, realistic light bouncing between the surfaces. No neon. No unnecessary dark areas.
**CAMERA:** 35mm environmental fashion photography for the overall scene, with natural perspective, coherent scale and perfect physical integration.
**IDENTITY:** Preserve exactly the same face, nose, beard, skin tone, jawline and recognisable personality from the uploaded reference in all three figures.
Each version has distinctly different sunglasses, hairstyle, outfit, posture and head direction;
