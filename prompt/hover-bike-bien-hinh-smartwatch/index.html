Hover Bike Biến Hình Smartwatch

Video 10s dọc: hoverbike xanh đêm hạ cánh trước tòa nhà, biến hình cơ học thành smartwatch bạc tự đeo vào cổ tay rider.

Nội dung prompt

Image-to-Video Prompt (10 Seconds)

Theme

Futuristic Corporate Sci-Fi — A Hover Bike Transforms into a Smartwatch

Style

Hyper-realistic cinematic sci-fi, premium corporate futurism, seamless mechanical transformation, realistic physics, ultra-detailed metallic surfaces, cyberpunk-lite, blockbuster VFX.

Camera

One continuous cinematic shot:

- Wide tracking shot following the hoverbike.
- Smooth medium shot during landing.
- Macro close-up during transformation.
- Rear tracking shot following the character into the building.

---

0.0–2.0s

The midnight-blue hoverbike glides silently above city traffic, its glowing cyan hover rings leaving faint energy trails. It slows gracefully and lands beneath the corporate entrance canopy while pedestrians stop and begin recording with their phones.

Audio: Soft anti-gravity hum, futuristic wind whoosh, distant city ambience.

---

2.0–4.0s

The rider steps off confidently, adjusts the suit jacket, and taps the glowing silver smartwatch. A crisp digital confirmation tone echoes as blue energy pulses across the hoverbike.

Audio: Shoes on pavement, smartwatch chime, electronic activation pulse.

---

4.0–7.0s

The hoverbike begins an intricate mechanical transformation. Wheels, frame, hover rings, handlebars, and body panels fold inward with satisfying precision. Thousands of tiny mechanical parts collapse seamlessly until the entire vehicle becomes a sleek floating silver smartwatch with glowing cyan accents.

The compact smartwatch gently floats toward the rider's wrist and automatically locks into place with a satisfying magnetic click.

Audio: Precision mechanical folding, magnetic locks, futuristic servos, soft energy hum.

---

7.0–10.0s

The rider smiles confidently, checks the newly formed smartwatch, and walks toward the revolving glass doors. The camera follows from behind as pedestrians continue filming the unbelievable transformation. The skyscraper doors open automatically while the smartwatch emits a subtle cyan glow. End on a cinematic pull-back revealing the futuristic skyline.

Audio: Footsteps, automatic doors opening, ambient city sounds, amazed crowd murmurs, soft futuristic ambience.

---
