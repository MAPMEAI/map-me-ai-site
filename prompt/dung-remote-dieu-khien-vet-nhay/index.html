Dùng Remote Điều Khiển Vẹt Nhảy

Dùng remote TV điều khiển dàn vẹt nhảy theo nhiều dòng nhạc hài hước.

Nội dung prompt

First Frame Image Prompt:
A close-up, eye-level shot of four large parrots sitting side-by-side on a brown tufted leather couch. From left to right: an African Grey parrot, a vibrant Blue-and-Yellow Macaw, a bright Green Amazon parrot with a red patch on its forehead, and a white Sulphur-crested Cockatoo. The background consists of closed white horizontal window blinds with soft natural daylight filtering through. The birds are all looking toward the camera with curious expressions.

Image-to-Video Prompt:
Theme: A hand uses a TV remote to “control” three parrots into different musical dances, then the fourth bird breaks into a chaotic solo without any prompt.
Visuals: A brown leather couch, four colorful parrots lined up side-by-side (African Grey, Blue-and-Yellow Macaw, Green Amazon parrot, Sulphur-crested Cockatoo), closed white horizontal blinds in the background, a hand holding a black remote control entering from the bottom of frame and pointing at each bird in turn.
Camera: Static medium shot, eye-level, focused on the birds on the couch.
Style: Casual home-video feel, bright natural indoor lighting, playful pet content, warm and humorous tone.
Action + Sound Design:
1. A hand holding a black TV remote enters from the bottom of the frame and points at the African Grey parrot on the far left — soft click sound.
2. A rhythmic hip-hop beat starts playing, and the African Grey parrot begins bobbing its head up and down to the beat.
3. The remote shifts to point at the Blue-and-Yellow Macaw, second from the left — soft click sound.
4. The music changes to a sweeping classical orchestral waltz, and the Macaw slowly and majestically lifts and lowers its large wings in time while the African Grey continues its head-bobbing.
5. The remote shifts to point at the Green Amazon parrot, third from the left — soft click sound.
6. The music switches to a fast-paced upbeat jazz instrumental, and the Green parrot rapidly bobs its head and sways side-to-side while the first two birds continue their own dances.
7. The hand lowers the remote out of frame, the music stops abruptly, and the first three birds immediately freeze still — silence.
8. After a brief pause, the Sulphur-crested Cockatoo on the far right spontaneously erupts into a wild dance on its own, raising its yellow crest, squawking loudly, and flapping its wings while jumping and head-banging — off-camera human laughter, no music.
