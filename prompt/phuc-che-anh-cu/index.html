PROMPT 1: PHỤC CHẾ ẢNH CŨ CÓ MÀU – GIỮ NGUYÊN 100% DANH TÍNH GỐC
PROMPT PHỤC CHẾ ẢNH CŨ CÓ MÀU – TƯƠI SÁNG, SẮC NÉT, GIỮ NGUYÊN 100% DANH TÍNH GỐC

Restore and professionally recover this old, damaged, torn, faded, blurry, scratched, low-resolution photograph into a fresh, clean, sharp, realistic color photo while preserving the original identity with absolute accuracy.

MAIN OBJECTIVE:
Transform the old damaged photograph into a high-resolution, photorealistic restored color image that looks fresh, bright, clean, sharp, natural, emotionally warm, and newly captured, while remaining faithful to the original photo.

This is a professional photo restoration and tasteful colorization task.
Restore the photograph beautifully, but do not redesign the people.

ABSOLUTE IDENTITY PRESERVATION:
Preserve every person exactly as in the original image.
The face must remain the same person.

Maintain 100% original:
face shape, forehead, eyes, eyelids, eyebrows, eyelashes, nose, nostrils, lips, teeth, ears, cheekbones, jawline, chin, facial proportions, facial asymmetry, expression, gaze direction, age, skin texture, wrinkles, natural marks, hairstyle, hairline, body shape, pose, posture, and emotional feeling.

DO NOT:
change the face
change facial identity
change the eyes
change the nose
change the mouth
change the smile
change the jawline
change the chin
change the hairstyle
change the hairline
make the person younger
make the person more attractive
beautify the face
smooth the skin unnaturally
create a different person
replace the face
change ethnicity
change age
change body proportions
change pose
change the original facial expression

IMPORTANT RESTORATION RULE:
Restore and clarify existing facial details only.
When facial areas are damaged or unclear, reconstruct conservatively based on the visible original structure.
Do not invent new beauty features.
Do not make the restored face look like a modern AI model.
Slight natural imperfection is better than changing the identity.

COLORIZATION STYLE:
Create a realistic, fresh, bright, harmonious color version.
Use tasteful natural color grading, not dull sepia.
The image should look clean, lively, and newly preserved, not old yellow, muddy, or overly brown.

Skin tone:
natural Vietnamese / Asian skin tone
healthy but realistic
not too pale
not too orange
not too red
not overly smooth
preserve natural pores and age details

Clothing color:
Colorize clothing creatively but realistically within the original clothing form.
You may choose harmonious, historically believable colors such as:
army green, olive green, moss green, navy blue, deep blue, light blue, beige, cream, white ivory, warm brown, soft gray, muted pastel, or natural fabric tones.

If the clothing looks like a uniform, workwear, military shirt, school shirt, or simple vintage shirt, choose a suitable color that makes the image brighter and more beautiful while still believable.
For military-style or old uniform clothing, olive green, army green, khaki, or muted green are preferred.
For simple shirts, use white ivory, light beige, pale blue, soft cream, or muted pastel if suitable.
Do not use random flashy colors.
Do not use neon colors.
Do not create colorful patterns unless the original photo clearly shows patterns.
Do not redesign the clothing shape.
Do not add accessories.
Do not add badges, medals, jewelry, buttons, or decorations that are not visible in the original.

Background color:
Preserve the original background and composition.
Colorize the background softly and naturally.
Use warm beige, soft gray, light brown, muted studio tones, natural wall tones, or gentle outdoor tones depending on the original image.
The background should support the subject, not overpower the face.
Do not replace the background.
Do not create a new location.
Do not add scenery that is not in the original photo.

LIGHTING AND FRESHNESS:
Make the photo brighter, cleaner, and more modern-looking while preserving the original lighting direction.
Improve natural light, soft highlights, gentle contrast, and depth.
The final image should feel like a fresh restored portrait, not a fantasy painting.
Avoid harsh HDR.
Avoid overexposure.
Avoid overly smooth studio retouching.

RESTORATION TASKS:
Carefully remove:
scratches, cracks, stains, dust, mold marks, torn paper damage, faded patches, scanning artifacts, compression noise, white spots, dark spots, blur, old photo degradation, yellow stains, uneven tones, and dirty texture.

Recover:
facial details, eye clarity, hair texture, skin texture, clothing texture, fabric folds, collar details, natural shadows, highlights, contours, edges, and tonal depth.

Enhance:
sharpness, clarity, micro-details, dynamic range, color harmony, realistic contrast, and natural depth.

MISSING OR DAMAGED AREAS:
Repair missing or damaged sections carefully.
Only reconstruct what is logically supported by the visible parts of the original image.
If a detail is unclear, keep it simple, natural, and conservative.
Do not create unsupported new facial features.
Do not change the person’s bone structure.

IMAGE QUALITY:
Ultra high-resolution restoration.
Photorealistic.
Fresh and sharp.
Bright but natural.
Clean but not plastic.
Museum-grade photo restoration.
Archival-quality recovery.
Natural skin texture.
Realistic hair texture.
Realistic fabric texture.
Natural color balance.
No AI-generated look.
No beauty filter.
No glamour retouching.
No fantasy details.
No artistic reinterpretation.

FINAL RESULT:
The final image must look like a beautifully restored, fresh, sharp, realistic color version of the original photograph, as if the photo were professionally preserved and newly captured today, while keeping 100% of the original identity, facial structure, age, expression, hairstyle, clothing form, pose, composition, and background.

PRIORITY SETTINGS:
Identity preservation priority: 100/100
Facial accuracy priority: 100/100
Original structure preservation priority: 100/100
Restoration quality priority: 100/100
Beautiful natural colorization priority: 95/100
Fresh and bright visual quality priority: 95/100
Creative clothing color within realistic limits: allowed
Creative generation priority: 10/100
Face replacement: forbidden
Face beautification: forbidden
Face redesign: forbidden
Age change: forbidden
Hairstyle change: forbidden
Clothing shape redesign: forbidden
Background replacement: forbidden
