Miniature Luxury Spa

Video 8s dựng phòng tắm miniature sang trọng từng chi tiết, phong cách ASMR thỏa mãn.

Miễn phíVideoVideo

Nội dung prompt

Duration: Exactly 8 seconds | Aspect Ratio: 9:16 vertical | Style: Ultra-realistic miniature construction, luxury architectural editorial, satisfying ASMR

Create a highly detailed physical miniature bathroom diorama assembled by realistic human hands on a scale-model platform. Every component must look genuinely handcrafted, with believable miniature proportions, material textures, contact shadows, and precise placement.

0:00–0:01 — THE EMPTY SHELL: Begin immediately with a bare miniature bathroom featuring warm travertine-textured walls and a grey stone floor. Hands place a sculptural oval white bathtub precisely against the back wall.

0:01–0:02 — BRASS INSTALLATION: Hands attach miniature brushed-gold shower controls, a rainfall showerhead, and a matching tub filler. Every fixture aligns naturally with the wall.

0:02–0:03 — THE FLOATING VANITY: Install a walnut floating vanity with a white stone basin and tiny gold faucet. Show a satisfying, precise final adjustment.

0:03–0:04 — THE MIRROR MOMENT: Carefully mount a round, thin gold-framed mirror above the basin. Its reflection matches the miniature room and lighting realistically.

0:04–0:05 — DESIGNER DETAILS: Place a neatly folded cream towel, a tiny textured beige rug, and a small green plant in a marble pot beside the bathtub.

0:05–0:06 — THE SPA FINISH: Add a narrow strip of concealed warm LED lighting beneath the vanity and along a wall recess. The lights activate naturally, illuminating the stone textures.

0:06–0:08 — THE REVEAL: Hands leave the frame. The camera makes a very subtle, smooth push-in toward the completed miniature spa. Warm light reflects across the brass fixtures and stone surfaces. End on a perfectly composed luxury architectural reveal.

CAMERA: Vertical 9:16, macro-level realism, smooth top-down isometric opening transitioning naturally into close-up details, stable camera, no abrupt cuts, no sudden zoom, no impossible camera movement. Keep the entire bathroom layout consistent.

LIGHTING & MATERIALS: Warm architectural lighting, realistic travertine, matte white ceramic, brushed brass, walnut grain, natural fabric fibers, accurate miniature reflections, shallow but believable depth of field. Premium interior-design commercial quality.

ASMR AUDIO: Soft ceramic placement, delicate metallic clicks, tiny wooden contact sounds, gentle rug placement, and subtle switch clicks as the LEDs turn on. No narration, music, or exaggerated sound effects.

CONTINUITY LOCK: Same room, scale, bathtub position, wall layout, fixtures, vanity, mirror, and lighting direction throughout. Each object appears only when the hands install it. Objects must not teleport, duplicate, deform, or change position after placement.

NEGATIVE PROMPT: Cartoon, toy-like plastic appearance, low-detail textures, obvious CGI, oversized hands, extra fingers, floating objects, teleporting fixtures, changing room layout, inconsistent scale, warped reflections, flickering lights, random objects appearing, messy installation, jump cuts, shaky camera, text, logos, watermark.

FINAL GOAL: A believable handcrafted miniature luxury spa that transforms from an empty shell into a polished architectural masterpiece in eight satisfying seconds.
