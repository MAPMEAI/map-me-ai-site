MASTER PROMPT — CINEMATIC WOOD ENGRAVING & GOLD INLAY TRANSFORMATION
You are an expert AI video generation system specializing in ultra-realistic woodworking, precision engraving, metallic gold inlay, and satisfying craftsmanship videos.
TASK:
Use the uploaded reference image as the primary visual reference and transform it into a highly realistic, cinematic woodworking process video. Preserve the main subject, design, composition, and visual identity of the uploaded image as closely as possible.
VIDEO SEQUENCE:
Scene 1 — Precision Engraving:
Show a close-up of skilled hands wearing black protective gloves using a precision engraving tool on a beautiful, dark walnut wooden board. The tool carefully carves intricate lines and fine decorative details into the wood, following the exact design of the uploaded reference image. Tiny wood shavings accumulate naturally around the carving.
Scene 2 — Molten Gold Application:
A small metal pouring vessel carefully pours glowing, molten golden metal into the engraved grooves. The gold flows naturally through the carved channels, filling the intricate design with brilliant metallic reflections. The liquid movement must look physically realistic and controlled.
Scene 3 — Surface Finishing:
Show a skilled craftsperson using a flat metal scraper to remove excess gold from the wooden surface. The engraved gold design gradually becomes clean, sharp, smooth, and beautifully defined, with realistic highlights and shadows.
Scene 4 — Polishing:
A professional handheld polishing tool gently polishes the wooden artwork. The surface becomes smooth and refined, revealing rich walnut grain and brilliant gold details. Fine dust particles and subtle tool movements create a satisfying craftsmanship experience.
Scene 5 — Final Reveal:
The finished wooden artwork is lifted carefully by black-gloved hands and tilted slightly toward the camera. Reveal an elegant, intricate gold-inlaid design with crisp engraved lines, a polished metallic finish, and beautiful natural wood grain. End with a steady close-up hero shot of the completed artwork.
VISUAL STYLE:
Ultra-photorealistic craftsmanship, cinematic macro photography, professional workshop lighting, realistic walnut wood texture, reflective molten gold, physically accurate material behavior, intricate engraving details, natural hand movements, shallow depth of field, smooth camera motion, premium luxury-art aesthetic, highly satisfying ASMR-style visuals, crisp 4K detail.
CAMERA:
Use close-up and macro shots, subtle camera movements, realistic focus transitions, and smooth transitions between the crafting stages. Keep the artwork clearly visible throughout the video.
ENVIRONMENT:
A clean professional craft workstation with a green work mat, realistic tools, soft directional lighting, and a dark wooden workpiece.
STRICT REFERENCE RULES:
- Use the uploaded image as the main visual reference.
- Preserve the original subject and recognizable design.
- Do not randomly change the artwork during the process.
- Maintain consistent wood color, engraving patterns, and gold appearance.
- Keep the hands, tools, and materials anatomically and physically realistic.
- Show believable progression from engraving to the finished gold-inlaid artwork.
- Avoid magical transformations, sudden unexplained changes, and unrealistic liquid behavior.
- Do not add text, logos, watermarks, or unrelated objects.
VIDEO FORMAT:
Vertical 9:16, 10 seconds, ultra-realistic cinematic video, smooth motion, high detail, professional macro cinematography.
