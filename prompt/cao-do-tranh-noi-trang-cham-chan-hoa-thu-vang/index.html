IMAGE PROMPT:
A sculptural white bas-relief artwork mounted on a smooth warm light-grey plaster wall, shot straight from the front under soft diffused daylight. Slightly left of centre sits a photorealistic red fox in profile facing right: warm orange-rust fur, cream chest and throat, dark charcoal legs and ear tips, a full brush tail curled around its paws, small black nose, alert eyes. The fox is the only element in full natural colour; everything around it is pure ivory white. Two sculpted flowering trees frame the scene - the left one leans inward with a dense canopy of hand-cut blossoms, layered petals and knotted roots, the right one mirrors it slightly shorter. Between and below them flow thick sweeping ribbons of white sculpted material, glossy palette-knife plaster and stacked cardstock ridges, surging from the lower right toward the centre like a frozen river. Fine sculpted grasses, ferns and tiny blossoms line the edges. Deep soft self-shadows reveal the tactile relief: visible layered edges, slight drop shadows, matte chalky surface with a subtle sheen along the ridges. Wide panoramic spacing, generous empty wall space across the top and right, balanced asymmetric layout. Monochrome palette of ivory, bone white and cool grey shadow with a single warm amber-rust accent from the fox. Museum-quality handcrafted diorama, ultra-detailed, high resolution, no text, no letters, no signature, no watermark, no frame, no border, artwork bleeding to all edges

VIDEO PROMPT:
3D paper cut art style, layered paper sculpture aesthetic, volumetric cotton-wool and felted-fibre texture with soft fluffy wisps along every cut edge. Ivory-white sculpted relief world: a large blossom-covered paper tree at the upper left, a small sculpted blossom tree at the right edge, carved paper waves flowing across the foreground, scattered paper flowers and pale rocks, all in cream and pale grey. A single red fox sits on a white paper rock at the left of the frame. The fox lifts its head, rises onto its paws and begins to walk to the right along the sculpted ledge. The instant its first paw touches the paper ground, warm golden light ignites beneath the paws and bursts outward as a smooth expanding ring, pouring golden light into the carved grooves of the relief, golden sparks and glowing dust lifting from the ground. Where the light passes, the ivory paper takes colour: the carved waves deepen into amber and russet, blossoms turn from white to autumn orange, gold and warm crimson, trunks warm to caramel, and real autumn leaves detach from the paper branches and drift slowly down, fluttering. The colour spreads as a wave travelling left to right behind the walking fox, and the last ivory corner at the right edge turns gold as it goes. Camera tracks slowly right with the fox, gentle parallax between sculpted layers, warm autumn palette against ivory, cinematic lighting, smooth magical motion
