Vườn Nhà Việt — Viral Camera

Video 10s dọc: camera lướt liên tục qua vườn rau nhà Việt (cà chua, ớt, giàn bầu bí, ao cá, mèo cam) kết bằng wide shot nhà gỗ.

Nội dung prompt

PROMPT VIDEO 10S – VIRAL REAL CINEMA**

Create a highly engaging 10-second vertical viral video, 9:16, 1080x1920, 4K ultra-detailed, realistic cinema camera footage, photorealistic, natural Vietnamese home garden.

The video must feel ALIVE, dynamic and visually addictive from the very first frame.

**OPENING 0–2s — STRONG HOOK:**
Start extremely close to a large ripe red tomato and bright chili peppers in the foreground. The camera quickly pushes forward through the leaves, creating a dramatic reveal into the lush garden behind. Leaves naturally brush past the lens, creating strong foreground motion and depth.

**2–5s — FAST REVEAL:**
The camera smoothly but energetically moves sideways and forward through the garden pathway, revealing multiple layers of vegetables one after another: ripe tomatoes, glossy eggplants, red chili peppers, fresh lettuce, mustard greens and long beans. Plants gently sway in the breeze. Several ripe vegetables are prominently visible close to the camera.

**5–8s — SURPRISE MOMENT:**
The camera suddenly curves around a wooden trellis covered with gourds, bottle gourds and sponge gourds, revealing a small beautiful pond beside the garden. A few colorful fish quickly swim across the water. An orange cat naturally walks into the frame and briefly looks toward the camera before continuing along the pathway.

**8–10s — SATISFYING FINAL REVEAL:**
The camera rises slightly while continuing forward, revealing the entire lush home garden beside a charming wooden countryside house. Warm sunlight shines through the leaves. End with a visually satisfying wide shot showing the abundance of vegetables, fruit trees, pond and house.

**CAMERA MOVEMENT:**
Continuous energetic cinematic camera movement throughout the entire 10 seconds. Combination of fast push-in, smooth lateral tracking, close foreground pass-through, gentle curved movement around plants, then a short rising reveal. No static shots. No long pauses. Every 1–2 seconds should introduce a new visual detail.

Use realistic gimbal movement with subtle handheld imperfection. Strong parallax between foreground leaves, middle-ground vegetables and background house. Natural motion blur during camera movement.

**ENVIRONMENT:**
A real Vietnamese home garden directly beside/behind a countryside house, NOT an open field. Compact productive garden with narrow pathways, wooden trellises, raised beds and natural landscaping.

Include chili peppers, lemon trees, okra, eggplants, tomatoes, lettuce, mustard greens, long beans, gourds, bottle gourds, sponge gourds and papaya trees. Plants must look naturally grown with slight imperfections, different sizes and realistic foliage.

**LIGHTING:**
Beautiful warm natural morning sunlight, sun rays occasionally passing through leaves, realistic shadows, natural exposure, subtle highlights, cinematic depth.

**VIRAL VISUAL STYLE:**
Immediate visual hook.
Constant movement.
Strong foreground-to-background transitions.
Multiple visual discoveries.
Rich green natural environment.
Satisfying reveal at the end.
The viewer should feel like they are physically walking through a beautiful hidden home garden.

Make it look like authentic footage captured by a professional cinema camera, NOT AI-generated.

NO people.
NO text.
NO captions.
NO logo.
NO watermark.
NO voice.
NO music.
NO artificial-looking plants.
NO fantasy elements.
NO open farmland.
NO static composition.
NO slow motion.
NO excessive saturation.
NO unrealistic camera movement.
NO duplicated objects.

Every new generation must use a completely different camera path, plant arrangement, foreground object, reveal sequence and visual composition while maintaining the same realistic Vietnamese home-garden concept. Do not repeat previous generations.
