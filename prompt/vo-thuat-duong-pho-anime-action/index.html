Võ Thuật Đường Phố (Anime Action)

Nội dung prompt

LLeft character: Black-haired teen, red plaid jacket, white T-shirt, dark pants. Combat style: power-based, oppressive, close-quarters brawling.
Right character: Silver-blue short-haired teen, black-and-white tech jacket, dark pants, red-and-white sneakers. Combat style: high-speed rush, agile, blue-energy-enhanced.
Setting: Dense modern urban street with tall buildings, Hong Kong-style neon signs, red taxis, strong-perspective street views.
Overall style: High-polish hot-blooded Chinese animation / cinematic anime movie feel / ultra-high-energy battle PV.
Core requirements: 15 seconds, 16:9, explosive from first second, extremely fast moves, dynamic camera tracking, exaggerated effects, strong speed sense, clear environmental destruction, maximize screen time for combat, no dialogue, subtitles, logos, or UI.

15-Second Action Design:
- 0–3s: Burst from first-frame pose. Silver-blue character throws a punch wreathed in massive blue current and glowing energy-fist aura, aimed head-on at black-haired character. Black-haired character squares shoulders and blocks head-on, left arm absorbing impact while right fist counter-punches. A huge blue-white shockwave erupts, sending ground water, gravel, and debris exploding outward. Red taxi shakes violently. Camera starts tight on fists, then whips around both in a drone-like half-circle arc.
- 3–6s: Black-haired unleashes close-pressure: straight punches, elbow strikes, knee strikes, shoulder checks with white explosive fist-wind. Silver-blue blocks, weaves, sidesteps with blue afterimages. Black-haired punches Silver-blue into a red taxi, denting the door. Silver-blue vaults over black-haired, leaving a long blue energy trail, and lands with a spinning roundhouse kick.
- 6–9s: Silver-blue shifts to high-speed counteroffensive: rapid circles with straight punches, elbow strikes, and heavy energy punches like fluid lightning. Heavy blow sends black-haired crashing into a street structure with exploding debris. Camera slides low and fast on the ground.
- 9–12s: Black-haired grabs Silver-blue’s wrist, forcibly breaking rhythm. Ground fractures. Black-haired follows with a heavy combo: abdominal punch, uppercut, elbow, and final heavy blow sending opponent flying into a sign.
- 12–15s: Final climax. Both engage in an ultra-high-speed exchange of consecutive straight-punch collisions, cross-blocks, and spin kicks. Black-haired lands a massive straight punch sending Silver-blue sliding back on one knee. Camera quickly passes between them, ending on a high-tension standoff amidst the devastated street.
