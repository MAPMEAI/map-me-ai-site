BỘ PROMPT VIDEO TRANH CÁT — CHỦ ĐỀ 20/11: TRI ÂN THẦY CÔ QUA CA KHÚC “NGƯỜI LÁI ĐÒ”
Gồm 10 prompt độc lập (cảnh 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6A, 6B, 7, 8, 9), mỗi prompt 8 giây, dùng riêng lẻ được: mỗi prompt đã có đủ khóa phong cách, quy tắc biểu diễn, âm thanh và định dạng.

============================
CẢNH 1 — NGƯỜI LÁI ĐÒ
============================
STYLE & CONSISTENCY LOCK: Authentic live sand-art performance on an illuminated lightbox, viewed directly from overhead. Real dark sand grains on a warm amber/sepia glowing surface; visible finger sweeps, palm smudges, grain accumulation, soft eroded edges and natural sand movement. Hand-drawn hollow-centered silhouettes with expressive sand contours and airy negative space. Keep the same teacher/ferryman design, student silhouette language, proportions, contour softness and sand density throughout the sequence. The artist's hands must visibly create, erase and reshape the image in real time. No CGI, no 3D cartoon, no digital illustration, no live-action setting. No on-screen text, subtitles, logos or watermark.
PERFORMANCE RULE: Reveal each visual element only as its matching voice-over phrase is heard. Do not show the finished composition at the beginning. Use believable continuous sand manipulation; no jump cuts or objects appearing by themselves.
AUDIO: Warm, gentle Vietnamese voice-over in a natural Southern Vietnamese accent; soft emotional piano underneath and subtle real sand-brushing sounds. Keep the narration clear and synchronized.
FORMAT: 8 seconds, 16:9, fixed overhead top-down camera, premium handcrafted sand-art performance, clean composition.

CHARACTERS: A humble Vietnamese teacher represented by a hollow-centered sand silhouette, gently rowing a small wooden ferry.
COMPOSITION: A wide river flows horizontally across the lightbox; warm amber light glows through thin sand.
ACTION: The artist first sweeps flowing river ripples across the frame. As the narration begins, fingers outline the narrow wooden boat, then shape the teacher holding an oar. Short diagonal finger scratches appear around the boat to symbolize alternating rain and sunlight. Keep the boat and ferryman readable and central.
TRANSITION: A continuous finger sweep carries the river current toward the next composition.
EMOTION: Quiet dedication, tenderness and gratitude.
VOICE-OVER: “Thầy cô sớm tối âm thầm lái đò, nắng mưa một đời đưa khách sang sông.”
