The Aurora Queen and Her Dragons

Ảnh chân dung fantasy nữ hoàng cực quang cùng 2 chú rồng, khung dọc 9:16.

Miễn phíẢnhẢnh

Nội dung prompt

Aspect Ratio: 9:16 vertical

Visual Style: Ultra-realistic cinematic fantasy, luxury editorial portrait, extraordinary micro-detail, 8K-quality rendering.

Create a breathtaking, hyper-realistic close-up fantasy portrait of an ethereal queen bonded with two magnificent pearlescent dragons inside a luminous crystalline kingdom.

THE AURORA QUEEN

A beautiful adult queen with luminous natural skin, captivating turquoise-blue eyes, delicate facial features, and long, flowing midnight-black hair infused with teal, aqua, and violet highlights. Her hair moves naturally through the magical atmosphere.

She wears an extraordinary platinum-and-gold royal crown intricately adorned with opals, sapphires, tiny diamonds, and iridescent crystal spires. Matching gemstone drop earrings, layered pearl necklaces, and delicate jeweled accessories enhance her regal elegance.

Her luxurious teal-blue couture gown features intricate silver embroidery, crystalline floral motifs, shimmering translucent fabric, and sculptural feather-like details that reflect the colors of the aurora.

THE MAGNIFICENT PRISMATIC DRAGON

On the left side of the composition, a majestic adult dragon leans affectionately toward the queen. Its anatomically coherent head features expressive sapphire-blue eyes, a powerful yet elegant snout, finely sculpted facial anatomy, and thousands of individually detailed pearlescent scales.

Its scales reflect iridescent teal, lavender, pale pink, silver, and champagne-gold highlights. Translucent crystalline horns and delicate luminous ridges crown its head.

The queen gently cups the dragon's jaw with one hand, while their faces rest close together in a tender, emotionally engaging moment of trust and affection.

THE BABY AURORA DRAGON

Nestled securely in the queen's arms is an adorable miniature dragon with large expressive turquoise eyes, a delicate pearlescent snout, tiny crystalline horns, small translucent wings, and shimmering opalescent scales.

Its little claws rest naturally against the queen's gown. Its wings display subtle rainbow refractions, while its curious expression creates an immediate emotional connection with the viewer.

The baby dragon must look like a believable miniature version of the adult dragon, with consistent scale patterns, coherent anatomy, and realistic proportions.

ENVIRONMENT & CINEMATIC LIGHTING

The background reveals an enchanted crystalline palace with towering translucent ice formations, softly blurred architectural spires, floating luminous particles, and distant aurora-like ribbons of teal, aqua, violet, and pink light.

Soft cinematic key lighting illuminates the queen's face. Cool blue rim lighting outlines the dragons, while delicate champagne-gold highlights create luxurious contrast. Realistic subsurface scattering, physically plausible reflections, intricate scale textures, natural skin detail, soft atmospheric mist, and shallow depth of field create extraordinary visual realism.

COMPOSITION & QUALITY

Vertical 9:16 portrait, intimate close-up framing, queen positioned slightly right of center, adult dragon filling the left side, baby dragon clearly visible in the lower center. Keep all three faces readable, beautifully proportioned, and sharply focused.

Create a powerful visual hook through the adult dragon's luminous sapphire eye, the queen's captivating gaze, and the baby dragon's iridescent wings. Preserve natural hand anatomy, realistic contact between the queen and both dragons, elegant negative space, and a sophisticated luxury-fantasy aesthetic.

Ultra-detailed materials, cinematic color grading, realistic reflections, exquisite craftsmanship, premium fantasy art direction, extraordinary depth, polished photographic realism, masterpiece quality.

NEGATIVE PROMPT

cartoon, anime, illustration, plastic skin, artificial beauty filter, low resolution, blurry faces, oversaturated neon colors, flat lighting, generic fantasy composition, malformed hands, extra fingers, missing fingers, fused fingers, extra limbs, duplicate dragons, multiple heads, distorted anatomy, asymmetrical eyes, cross-eyed characters, malformed dragon snout, broken wings, excessive spikes, inconsistent scales, floating body parts, unnatural touching, awkward poses, duplicated jewelry, melted crown, tangled accessories, harsh shadows, excessive bloom, muddy colors, noisy textures, text, typography, logo, watermark, border, cropped faces, out-of-frame baby dragon.
