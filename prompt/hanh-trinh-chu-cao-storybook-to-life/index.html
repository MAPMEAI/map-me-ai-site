Hành Trình Chú Cáo (Storybook to Life)

Chạm tay vào bìa sách thiếu nhi làm chú cáo bước ra đời thực.

Miễn phíVideoVideo

Nội dung prompt

First Frame Image Prompt:
A charming vintage children's storybook cover titled "Adventures of Mr Fox" displayed flat on a rustic wooden table surface. The book has a decorative cover with ornate cream-colored lettering for "Adventures of" and bold orange-gold lettering for "Mr Fox", surrounded by small leafy flourishes. The central illustration shows an anthropomorphic red fox wearing a brown explorer's coat, an orange scarf, and carrying a small satchel, walking confidently along a winding forest path between tall trees. The background depicts a warm autumn woodland with soft hills in the distance, ferns, and fallen leaves. Around the book on the wooden table are natural adventuring props: a brass compass, dried oak leaves, acorns, a sprig of berries, and a wooden-handled fountain pen. Soft, warm lighting, cozy nostalgic atmosphere, portrait orientation.

Image-to-Video Prompt:
A realistic hand enters from the right, and when the fingertip gently taps the illustrated fox on the book cover, the fox magically comes to life as a 3D furry creature, jumps out of the flat book cover, and steps onto the open palm where it curls up cozily and falls asleep.
