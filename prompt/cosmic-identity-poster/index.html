Hyper-Premium Cinematic Cosmic Identity Poster | 4:5 Vertical | Ultra-High-Resolution 32K Style

CORE CONCEPT:

Create a breathtaking, ultra-photorealistic cinematic fantasy poster featuring two powerful figures standing back-to-back in a dramatic cosmic dimension: a confident male AI creator representing human creativity and a mysterious hooded shadow entity representing the unknown power of artificial intelligence.

CHARACTER 1 — THE CREATOR:

A stylish South Asian male creator, approximately 26–30 years old, with natural textured black hair, a neatly groomed beard, realistic warm skin, and black premium sunglasses. He wears a vivid electric-cobalt-blue luxury jacket with subtle gold embroidery, a slightly untucked ivory-white T-shirt, dark olive cargo trousers, and pristine white luxury high-top sneakers with metallic-gold accents. Arms crossed confidently, relaxed posture, powerful expression, realistic anatomy, detailed fabric stitching, natural skin texture, and editorial fashion-campaign quality.

CHARACTER 2 — THE SHADOW:

A towering, mysterious humanoid entity standing directly behind the creator, facing slightly away while maintaining a powerful back-to-back silhouette. Its face is completely concealed inside a deep black hood, with only two narrow electric-blue glowing eyes visible. The figure wears an intricate, distressed black cloak composed of layered, torn, feather-like fabric strips that flow dramatically through the cosmic wind. Obsidian-black textures, subtle metallic highlights, realistic folds, and an ominous yet sophisticated presence. Entirely original character design.

COSMIC ENVIRONMENT:

An enormous, molten-gold celestial sphere dominates the background, resembling a radiant ancient moon with intricate crater formations, glowing mineral textures, and fiery surface details. Deep midnight-black space surrounds the sphere, filled with electric-cobalt-blue nebula clouds, scattered floating asteroids, drifting cosmic debris, and elegant golden energy trails. The environment feels immense, cinematic, and physically dimensional rather than artificial or cartoonish.

COMPOSITION & CAMERA:

Full-body, low-angle cinematic portrait with both characters clearly visible from head to footwear. Position the creator in the left foreground and the shadow entity in the right background, their backs nearly touching. The golden celestial sphere frames both silhouettes without obscuring their faces or clothing. Strong foreground-to-background separation, precise anatomy, balanced visual weight, dramatic depth, and an unmistakable central focal point.

LIGHTING & COLOR SCIENCE:

Intense molten-gold backlighting creates luminous rim light around both figures. Electric-blue atmospheric illumination contrasts with the warm golden highlights. Deep obsidian shadows preserve the mysterious mood, while subtle reflected light reveals the creator's facial features, jacket texture, and the shadow's layered cloak. High dynamic range, cinematic volumetric lighting, realistic atmospheric scattering, controlled highlights, and rich blacks.

RENDERING QUALITY:

Photorealistic human anatomy, physically based materials, extraordinary micro-texture, realistic hair strands, detailed beard, natural skin pores, premium fashion photography, advanced cinematic CGI integration, sophisticated color grading, razor-sharp focal details, realistic depth of field, epic blockbuster-poster quality.

OUTPUT SPECIFICATIONS:

4:5 vertical composition, ultra-high-resolution 32K-style detail, full-body framing, cinematic luxury editorial finish, premium international audience appeal.

NEGATIVE PROMPT:

low resolution, blurry faces, cartoon, anime, plastic skin, excessive smoothing, incorrect anatomy, extra fingers, missing limbs, duplicate characters, floating body parts, distorted hands, malformed shoes, cropped feet, inaccurate facial proportions, washed-out gold, flat lighting, muddy shadows, excessive lens flare, cluttered composition, cheap fantasy aesthetic, generic robot, holographic gadgets, random text, oversized watermark, recognizable copyrighted characters, artificial-looking fabric, oversaturated colors, poor rendering, motion blur.
