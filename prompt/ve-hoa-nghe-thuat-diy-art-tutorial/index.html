Vẽ Hoa Nghệ Thuật (DIY Art Tutorial)

Video hướng dẫn vẽ cây hoa tử đằng bằng đáy chai nhựa.

Nội dung prompt

Theme: A relaxing DIY art tutorial showing how to paint a blooming wisteria tree using the bottom of a plastic bottle as a stamping tool to create textured flowers.
Visuals: Bright white art studio setting, a white canvas on a light wooden easel, thick vibrant purple acrylic paint, a clear plastic bottle with a flower-shaped bottom, fine and flat paintbrushes, a bare grey tree trunk transforming into a fully blossomed purple wisteria tree with sculpted impasto flowers, yellow centers, green leaves, and falling petals.
Camera: Tight close-up shots focused on hands and canvas, minimal camera movement, quick cuts between technique steps, shallow depth of field with soft white background.
Style: Clean, bright, satisfying DIY art tutorial aesthetic, soft and calming, vertical mobile format, photorealistic with emphasis on thick paint texture.
Action + Sound Design:
1. Close-up of the finished wisteria painting on the easel, a hand adds a final brushstroke — gentle acoustic guitar melody begins.
2. A hand dips the flower-shaped bottom of a clear plastic bottle into a pool of thick purple paint on a white palette, lifting it to reveal a paint-coated blossom shape — soft squishing paint sound.
3. The hand presses the paint-covered bottle onto the canvas where a bare grey tree trunk and branches are pre-painted, stamping a thick textured five-petaled purple flower onto a branch — satisfying wet paint stamping sound.
4. The stamping is repeated across the branches, building up clusters of purple and white blossoms one by one — repeated soft stamping sounds.
5. A fine-tip brush adds bright yellow dots to the center of each stamped flower, then dabs small green leaves around the blooms — gentle brush-on-canvas sounds.
6. A wider flat brush sweeps darker grey strokes onto the tree trunk, creating bark texture and depth — broad brushstroke sounds.
7. The camera pulls back to reveal the completed painting on the easel — a full wisteria tree covered in cascading purple and white impasto flowers — acoustic guitar melody fades out softly.
