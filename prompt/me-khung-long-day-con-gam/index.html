Mẹ Khủng Long Dạy Con Gầm

Hoạt hình 3D ấm áp cảnh mẹ khủng long T-Rex dạy con tập gầm.

Nội dung prompt

First Frame Image Prompt:
A wide eye-level cinematic shot in 3D animated feature-film CGI style, warm golden-hour cinematic lighting. A large, friendly adult green T-Rex-like dinosaur with soft stylized textures stands on the right side of the frame, smiling affectionately down at a tiny baby dinosaur of the same species on the left. The baby dinosaur looks up with innocent curiosity. Lush prehistoric jungle background filled with giant ferns, palm trees, rocky outcrops, and a distant waterfall, bathed in warm yellow-orange sunlight. Shallow depth of field blurs the jungle background. Heartwarming, family-friendly mood. Vertical composition, 9:16 aspect ratio.

Image-to-Video Prompt:
Theme: A heartwarming prehistoric jungle moment where a parent T-Rex teaches its tiny baby how to roar.
Camera: Wide establishing shot, then cuts to a close-up on the baby dinosaur’s face, then cuts back to the wide shot for the emotional payoff, gentle push-in during tender moments.
Style: 3D animated feature-film CGI, Pixar/DreamWorks-like, colorful and polished, soft cinematic lighting.
Action + Sound Design:
1. The large dinosaur smiles down affectionately at the tiny baby dinosaur standing before it — gentle jungle ambience, distant waterfall, birds chirping.
2. The large dinosaur opens its mouth wide and lets out a powerful roar — deep thunderous dinosaur roar, flock of birds flying past in the background sky, wind rustling through leaves.
3. Close-up on the baby dinosaur: it scrunches up its face, puffs out its cheeks, and opens its tiny mouth to attempt a roar — small adorable squeaky roar, soft breathing.
4. Wide shot: the large dinosaur looks surprised, then its expression softens into a warm, gentle smile — low affectionate rumble.
5. The large dinosaur lowers its massive head and gently nudges the baby dinosaur in a tender display of love — soft affectionate purr-like rumble, peaceful jungle ambience.
