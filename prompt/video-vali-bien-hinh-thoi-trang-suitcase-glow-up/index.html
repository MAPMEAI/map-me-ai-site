Video Vali Biến Hình Thời Trang — The Suitcase Glow-Up

Prompt video 10s dọc 9:16 (Flow/Veo): vali du lịch mở ra và biến hình thành set thời trang hoàn chỉnh cho người mẫu — hiệu ứng glow-up mượt mà, phong cách quảng cáo cao cấp.

Nội dung prompt

Create an exactly 10-second, vertical 9:16, high-quality stylized fashion-doll transformation video. Set the scene in a bright premium fashion studio with a clean pastel backdrop, polished floor, soft cinematic lighting, and a playful luxury editorial aesthetic.

0–2s — THE BEFORE: One stylish but messy-looking fashion-doll character stands beside one large elegant suitcase. Her hair is wildly unstyled, and her outfit is deliberately simple and untidy. She notices the suitcase and looks at it with playful curiosity.

2–3.5s — THE ENTRY: The character naturally opens the suitcase, climbs inside, and disappears completely into it. Her movement must show clear physical contact, believable proportions, and natural body movement.

3.5–5.5s — THE MYSTERY: The suitcase closes naturally and remains physically stable for a brief moment. It gives one subtle, believable movement to build anticipation. No floating objects, magical effects, or unrealistic motion.

5.5–7.5s — THE TRANSFORMATION REVEAL: The suitcase opens naturally. Reveal the exact same character rising from inside, now completely transformed into a glamorous fashion icon with beautifully styled hair, polished makeup, and a sophisticated high-fashion outfit.

7.5–9s — THE FASHION MOMENT: She steps naturally out of the suitcase and makes one confident, elegant turn to show the complete transformed look. Her hair and clothing move naturally.

9–10s — FINAL HERO SHOT: End with the transformed character standing confidently beside the open suitcase in a premium fashion-editorial composition.

STYLE AND MOTION:

Smooth expressive stylized character animation, premium fashion-doll proportions, detailed natural hair and fabric movement, realistic suitcase interaction, consistent character identity before and after transformation, sophisticated luxury styling, clean composition, soft cinematic lighting, and polished high-fashion commercial quality.

AUDIO:

Upbeat, elegant fashion-pop instrumental with synchronized sounds: suitcase clasps opening and closing, soft footsteps, subtle suitcase movement, elegant fabric movement, and a satisfying transformation reveal accent.

NEGATIVE:

No text, logos, watermarks, subtitles, narration, extra characters, duplicate character, duplicate suitcase, magical particles, portals, floating objects, teleportation, distorted body, extra limbs, extra fingers, broken anatomy, sudden camera cuts, flickering, inconsistent character identity, low-quality CGI, or unrelated objects.

CONTINUITY:

Exactly ONE character and ONE suitcase throughout the entire video. The same character must remain instantly recognizable before and after the transformation. The transformation happens only while she is completely inside the closed suitcase.
