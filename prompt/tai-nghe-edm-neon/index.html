Nội dung prompt

10-second viral music video, vertical 9:16, 1080×1920, 4K, 60fps.

A premium black over-ear wireless headphone floats in the center of a futuristic neon night scene.

The headphone stays front-facing and clearly visible. Its orientation remains stable. The movement is a fast rhythmic bouncing and impact motion, synchronized tightly to the music: sharp downward hits, powerful upward rebounds, rapid repeated beats and strong bass impacts. The headphone should look like it is physically reacting to powerful bass, energetic and punchy, NOT gently floating.

MUSIC: extremely energetic viral EDM, 140–150 BPM, heavy deep bass, hard kick, punchy drums, fast percussion, catchy electronic melody and an explosive beat drop. Strong bass hits must visibly drive the headphone's movement.

Camera movement is fast and energetic but keeps the headphone centered. Neon lights pulse with the beat.

Premium realistic product commercial, visually addictive, high-energy viral style.

NO: spinning, 360° rotation, continuous rotation, slow floating, gentle movement, slow music, ambient music, vocals, dialogue, people, text, subtitles, logo, brand name, watermark, UI, stickers.
