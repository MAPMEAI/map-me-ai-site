Video Mở Hộp Máy Pha Espresso Mini — Pha Latte Art Tí Hon

Prompt video 10s dọc 9:16: mở hộp máy pha espresso mini tí hon và pha latte art ngay trên quầy bếp miniature — chi tiết máy thật, bọt sữa mịn như thật.

Nội dung prompt

Create a 10-second ultra-realistic, cinematic macro video in vertical 9:16 format, showing a miniature professional coffee shop setup with a tiny espresso machine.
SCENE 1 (0–2 seconds): UNBOXING
An extreme macro close-up of realistic human hands carefully placing a tiny rectangular brown cardboard package on a beautiful wooden coffee shop counter. The hands gently untie the white ribbon and use a small knife to open the miniature cardboard box. The movements are natural, delicate, and realistic.
SCENE 2 (2–4 seconds): REVEALING THE MACHINE
The hands slowly open the miniature cardboard packaging, revealing an incredibly detailed, professional miniature black-and-silver espresso machine inside. Carefully lift the tiny espresso machine out of the box and place it on the wooden counter.
SCENE 3 (4–5.5 seconds): PREPARATION
Close-up macro shot of the miniature espresso machine. Human hands gently wipe its surface with a tiny white cleaning cloth. Then, a hand picks up the miniature portafilter, fills it with freshly ground coffee, and carefully presses the coffee grounds using a tiny metal tamper.
SCENE 4 (5.5–7 seconds): MAKING ESPRESSO
An extreme close-up of the miniature portafilter being attached to the espresso machine. A tiny white ceramic espresso cup is placed underneath. The machine starts extracting rich, golden-brown espresso into the cup. Beautiful crema forms naturally on the surface.
SCENE 5 (7–8.5 seconds): MILK AND LATTE ART
A miniature stainless-steel milk pitcher is held by realistic human fingers. Fresh white steamed milk is poured slowly into the espresso cup. Create beautiful, detailed latte art in the shape of a delicate heart or rosette.
SCENE 6 (8.5–10 seconds): FINAL PRESENTATION
A cinematic macro hero shot reveals the completed miniature coffee shop setup. The tiny espresso machine stands on the wooden counter, with a perfectly prepared miniature latte and a small cookie on a wooden serving tray. A human hand gently places the coffee cup beside the machine.
VISUAL STYLE:
- Hyper-realistic miniature photography.
- Professional cinematic macro videography.
- Vertical 9:16 composition.
- Warm, cozy coffee shop atmosphere.
- Beautiful wooden countertop.
- Soft warm hanging Edison lights.
- Blurred coffee shop shelves in the background.
- Extremely shallow depth of field.
- Beautiful creamy bokeh.
- Natural realistic human fingers and skin textures.
- Highly detailed miniature espresso equipment.
- Smooth cinematic camera movements.
- Warm professional color grading.
- Realistic coffee extraction and milk pouring physics.
- Satisfying ASMR-style visual experience.
- Sharp focus on the miniature objects.
- Consistent miniature scale throughout the video.
IMPORTANT: Every object, including the espresso machine, cardboard packaging, coffee cups, milk pitcher, and coffee accessories, must look miniature. Human hands must appear realistically sized in comparison. Maintain the same coffee shop environment, machine design, lighting, and object continuity throughout all scenes.
No subtitles, no text overlays, no transitions, no cartoon effects, no unrealistic movements, and no extra fingers. Ultra-realistic professional cinematic quality.
