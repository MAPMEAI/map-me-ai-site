Đi Qua Nhiều Nước — Lip-sync Hài

Video 10s dọc: nhân vật đi bộ liên tục một shot, thoại Việt lip-sync liệt kê các loại 'nước' (tương, mắm, nước rửa chén, nước giặt, xả vải...) kết bằng punchline hài.

Nội dung prompt

Use the uploaded image as the EXACT main character. Keep the character's appearance unchanged.

Create a funny, energetic 10-second vertical 9:16 video with ONE continuous moving shot.

0–2s: The character walks confidently toward the camera, smiling and gesturing while talking.
2–7s: Still walking continuously, the character points left and right as soy sauce, fish sauce, dish soap, laundry detergent, fabric softener, floor cleaner and toilet cleaner pop up one after another. React with exaggerated funny expressions and hand gestures.
7–10s: The character keeps walking, turns toward the camera, raises both hands in a hilarious “really?” gesture and finishes the punchline.

Continuous body movement, hand gestures, facial expressions and smooth camera tracking throughout. Do NOT freeze, pose, or animate a still image with only blinking/breathing.

Vietnamese voice with natural lip-sync. Speak continuously from the first frame and finish ALL dialogue before the video ends:

“Tôi đã đi qua rất nhiều nước: nước tương, nước mắm, nước rửa chén, nước giặt, nước xả, nước lau nhà, nước chà toilet. Còn bạn, đi qua những nước nào rồi?”

No subtitles, text, logos, stickers or watermark.
