Video Hộp Quà Biến Hình Thời Trang — Gift Box Surprise Transformation

Prompt video 10s dọc 9:16 (Flow/Veo): hộp quà khổng lồ mở ra, người mẫu bước ra từ hộp với hiệu ứng biến hình thời trang cao cấp, chuyển cảnh mượt mà, ánh sáng studio sang trọng.

Miễn phíVideoVideo

Nội dung prompt

Create an exactly 10-second, vertical 9:16, high-quality stylized fashion-doll transformation video. Use a bright premium fashion-studio setting with a clean pastel backdrop, polished floor, soft cinematic lighting, and a playful luxury editorial aesthetic.

0–2s — THE BEFORE: A stylish but messy-looking fashion-doll character stands beside an oversized elegant gift box. Her hair is wildly unstyled, and her outfit is deliberately simple and chaotic. She looks curiously at the large beautifully wrapped gift box.

2–3.5s — THE JUMP: The character naturally opens the lid, climbs into the oversized gift box, and disappears completely inside. Her movement must have realistic physical contact and believable proportions.

3.5–5s — THE MYSTERY: The lid closes naturally. The closed gift box gives one subtle playful shake, creating anticipation. Keep the box physically stable with no floating or magical effects.

5–7s — THE REVEAL: The lid opens dramatically and the same character rises naturally from inside the box, now completely transformed into a glamorous premium fashion look with perfectly styled hair, polished makeup, and an elegant high-fashion outfit.

7–9s — THE FASHION MOMENT: She steps naturally out of the gift box, strikes one confident fashion pose, and smoothly turns to show the complete transformed look.

9–10s — FINAL HERO SHOT: Hold on the transformed character standing beside the open gift box in a premium editorial composition. End with a confident smile and a subtle natural movement of her hair and outfit.

STYLE AND MOTION:

Smooth expressive stylized character animation, premium fashion-doll proportions, detailed hair and fabric movement, realistic object interaction, consistent character identity before and after the transformation, clean studio composition, vibrant but sophisticated colors, soft cinematic shadows, polished luxury commercial quality.

AUDIO:

Upbeat playful fashion-pop instrumental with synchronized sounds: gift-box lid opening, soft footsteps, subtle box movement, elegant fabric movement, and a satisfying reveal accent.

NEGATIVE:

No text, logos, watermarks, subtitles, narration, extra characters, duplicate character, duplicate gift box, magical particles, floating objects, teleportation, distorted body, extra limbs, extra fingers, broken anatomy, sudden camera cuts, flickering, inconsistent character identity, low-quality CGI, or unrelated objects.

CONTINUITY:

Exactly ONE character and ONE gift box throughout the entire video. The same character must remain recognizable before and after the transformation. The transformation happens only while she is fully inside the closed gift box.
