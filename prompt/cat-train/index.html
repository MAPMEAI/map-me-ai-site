Cat Train

Mèo khổng lồ lai tàu hỏa được đút than tại sân ga Nhật Bản.

Miễn phíVideoVideo

Nội dung prompt

First Frame Image Prompt:
A photorealistic giant calico cat fused seamlessly with a passenger train carriage, resting its front paws on a small wheeled bogie on railway tracks at a Japanese-style outdoor train station platform. A station attendant in a dark blue uniform and cap stands on the platform in the foreground, holding a shovel loaded with black coal, ready to feed the cat-train. The cat has large, expressive yellow eyes and grey, white, and tan calico fur; its body transitions into a train car with passenger windows. Bright natural daylight, yellow tactile paving safety line on the platform, overhead station structures, blurred passengers waiting in the background, city buildings and a partly cloudy sky in the distance. Whimsical, surreal, cinematic live-action/CGI blend.

Image-to-Video Prompt:
Theme: A giant calico cat-train is fed coal by a station attendant at a Japanese railway platform, then stretches, whistles, and departs.
Visuals: Calico cat head as locomotive front, furry body merging into train carriages, glowing fiery furnace inside the cat’s open mouth, station attendant with coal shovel, steam shooting from the cat’s ears, yellow platform safety line, blurred commuters and city skyline behind.
Camera: Static wide shot from platform level, holding the cat-train and attendant in frame.
Style: Photorealistic CGI composited into live-action, bright natural daylight, playful and surreal Japanese train-station atmosphere.
Action + Sound Design:
[0-1.5s] The giant cat-train rests on the tracks while the attendant stands on the platform holding a shovel full of coal — ambient station ambience, faint distant announcements.
[1.5-4.5s] The attendant throws the coal into the cat’s open mouth, revealing a roaring orange furnace inside — shovel scrape, coal tumbling, fire crackle and whoosh.
[4.5-9.5s] The cat closes its mouth, licks its lips, lets out a satisfied meow, then stretches its front paws forward along the tracks and closes its eyes — soft meow, stretching creak, relaxed breath.
[9.5-11.5s] The cat returns to its resting position and steam bursts from both ears — sharp train whistle, loud steam hiss.
[11.5-15s] The cat-train begins rolling forward along the tracks, accelerating as it leaves the station — rhythmic wheel chugs, building engine roar, departing train momentum.
