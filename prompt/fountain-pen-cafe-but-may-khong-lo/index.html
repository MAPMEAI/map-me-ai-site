PROMPT — THE FOUNTAIN PEN CAFÉ

Create an ultra-realistic, cinematic surreal micro-art photograph of an extraordinary miniature luxury café built around an enormous elegant black fountain pen with refined championship-gold details. Vertical 4:5 composition, extreme macro photography, premium commercial editorial, ultra-high resolution, hyper-realistic.

THE GIANT FOUNTAIN PEN WORLD

A massive, beautifully crafted black-and-gold fountain pen rests diagonally across a luxurious polished warm wooden desk. The enormous fountain pen becomes an impossible miniature architectural world while remaining clearly recognizable as a realistic premium writing instrument.

Its ornate golden nib releases a stream of deep sapphire-blue ink onto the desk. The flowing ink naturally transforms into a glossy miniature river with realistic liquid reflections and surface tension. A beautiful miniature stone bridge crosses the blue ink river.

THE MINIATURE LUXURY CAFÉ

An elegant, highly detailed miniature café is seamlessly built around and beneath the giant fountain pen. The polished pen body forms the upper architectural structure, with sophisticated café terraces naturally integrated along its surface.

Tiny elegant people sit at miniature wooden café tables, enjoying coffee in a peaceful morning atmosphere. A tiny well-dressed businessman relaxes with an espresso cup while other miniature guests enjoy quiet conversations.

The café features warm golden interior lighting, refined walnut furniture, cream café umbrellas, striped awnings, subtle brass details, elegant shelves, miniature lamps, delicate flowers, climbing greenery and beautifully arranged plants.

BRANDING — [BRAND NAME]

Integrate the exact text "[BRAND NAME]" prominently but elegantly into the side of the giant fountain pen as a refined premium gold engraved logo. Add a small tasteful crown symbol above the branding.

Place a second subtle "[BRAND NAME]" text logo on a sophisticated miniature desk plaque in the foreground. The typography must be clean, sharp, correctly spelled and physically integrated into the objects.

THE SURROUNDING WORLD

The luxurious wooden desk contains oversized objects that emphasize the miniature scale: elegant stacked leather-bound books, a premium blue ink bottle, a sophisticated pen holder, scattered stationery and an open notebook.

Large windows behind the scene reveal a softly blurred modern city skyline during golden sunrise. Warm natural sunlight enters through the windows, creating cinematic reflections across the polished fountain pen, glossy blue ink river and miniature architecture.

PHOTOGRAPHY & QUALITY

Extreme macro photography, photorealistic miniature craftsmanship, physically accurate lighting, realistic liquid ink, natural reflections, ultra-detailed textures, cinematic shallow depth of field, sophisticated composition, warm atmospheric glow, premium luxury advertising photography, realistic scale relationships and natural human anatomy.

IMPORTANT: The giant fountain pen must remain the unmistakable centerpiece. The miniature café must look physically built around the pen with believable architecture and scale. No floating objects, no distorted anatomy, no cartoon style, no CGI appearance, no cyberpunk elements, no neon effects, no random text, no misspelled branding, no third-party logos, no watermark.

CÁCH ĐIỀN: [BRAND NAME] = tên thương hiệu của bạn (vd: tên shop/thương hiệu cá nhân của bạn) — chữ này sẽ được khắc vàng lên thân bút và khắc lại trên tấm bảng nhỏ ở tiền cảnh.
