Hair & Makeup: Relaxed, romantic half-up, half-down wave interwoven with subtle pearlescent hairpins. Fresh, dewy bridal makeup with natural bronze-and-gold highlights and a glossy nude-coral lip.

Wardrobe & Styling: A lightweight, cascading silk and tulle gown with a deeply embellished corset bodice, intricate floral vine embroidery, and delicate off-the-shoulder straps. Statement ocean-inspired pearl and gold filigree earrings. A bouquet of wild white roses, pampas grass, and sage foliage.

Environment & Lighting: An outdoor cliffside wedding venue overlooking a deep blue ocean, featuring a massive, asymmetrical floral arch bursting with white, peach, and coral roses, eucalyptus, and pampas grass. Crisp 5500K daylight with natural ocean reflections.

Technical Spec: Simulated Canon R5, 85mm lens, f/1.8, capturing the natural breeze and high-contrast environment. Creamy bokeh blurring the vast ocean and coastline behind the sharp subject.

Signature Finish: Apply professional color grading with high-key whites, deep vibrant saturation, and a clean, modern digital finish.
