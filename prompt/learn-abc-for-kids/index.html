Learn ABC for Kids

Video giáo dục 3D 10 cảnh học bảng chữ cái cho bé 2 tuổi, dọc 9:16.

Miễn phíVideoVideo

Nội dung prompt

**Create a high-quality vertical 9:16 3D animated educational kids video in a bright, colorful, cute nursery-rhyme style.**

**Main character:**

A cute adorable toddler boy, around 2 years old, with chubby cheeks, big expressive eyes, short light-brown hair, wearing a bright blue T-shirt and matching diaper/shorts. Keep the child’s appearance, voice, and proportions consistent throughout the entire video.

**Rule for every scene:**

In each scene, the toddler opens the door (or reaches the area), reacts with a clear, expressive facial and body reaction (surprise, joy, giggling, clapping, or pointing), then looks at the camera and says the featured word out loud in a sweet, clear toddler voice. The on-screen text appears at the top as he says the word.

**Environment:**

A clean, bright indoor children’s learning room with a glossy white floor, colorful geometric shapes in the background, playful decorations, and a series of colorful doors in vibrant red, blue, yellow, and green. Soft studio lighting, cheerful atmosphere, polished 3D cartoon animation, smooth reflections, Pixar-like children’s animation aesthetic.

**VIDEO SEQUENCE (10 scenes total):**

**Scene 1 — A for Apple:**

The toddler stands in front of a bright red door with a large bold yellow letter “A”. He reaches for the handle and opens the door with excitement. Behind it is a colorful apple-themed environment with red apples. Show the text “A - APPLE” at the top. The toddler looks surprised and happy, gasps, then says “Apple!”

**Scene 2 — B for Banana:**

Cut to a bright blue door with a large yellow “B”. The toddler walks toward it, reaches for the handle, and opens the door. Reveal a playful collection of bright yellow bananas. Display “B - BANANA” at the top. The toddler’s eyes go wide, he giggles and claps his hands, then says “Banana!”

**Scene 3 — C for Cat:**

Show a yellow/orange door with a large red “C”. The toddler opens the door and a cute friendly cat appears. Display “C - CAT”. The toddler reacts with excitement and curiosity, leaning forward with wide eyes, then says “Cat!”

**Scene 4 — D for Dog:**

Show a red door with a large yellow “D”. The toddler opens it and reveals a cute friendly brown-and-white puppy. The puppy happily looks toward the child. Display “D - DOG”. The toddler laughs, bounces with joy, then says “Dog!”

**Scene 5 — E for Elephant:**

Show a blue door with a large yellow “E”. The toddler opens the door and a cute baby elephant appears. The elephant playfully raises its trunk. Display “E - ELEPHANT”. The toddler gasps happily, claps, then says “Elephant!”

**Scene 6 — F for Fish:**

Transition to a bright underwater-themed aquarium area with colorful orange fish swimming in a large glass aquarium. The toddler stands beside it and looks excitedly at the fish, pressing his hands near the glass in amazement. Display “F - FISH”. He smiles wide, then says “Fish!”

**Scene 7 — G for Grapes:**

Show a green door with a large yellow “G”. The toddler opens the door and discovers a huge bunch of shiny purple grapes. Display “G - GRAPES”. He points at the grapes with excitement, his mouth open in delight, then says “Grapes!”

**Scene 8 — H for Horse:**

Show a yellow door with a large red “H”. The toddler opens the door and a cute friendly cartoon horse appears behind it. The horse looks gentle and playful, not scary, and nods its head happily toward the child. Display “H - HORSE”. The toddler gasps with delight, bounces with excitement, then says “Horse!”

**Scene 9 — I for Ice:**

Show a red door with a large yellow “I”. The toddler stands beside the closed door, reaches for the handle, and opens it. Reveal a playful icy environment with sparkling ice cubes and snowy shapes. Display “I - ICE”. He shivers playfully, hugs himself with a surprised smile, then says “Ice!”

**Scene 10 — Final scene (Bye-Bye):**

A cute fluffy hamster waves goodbye to the camera against a bright pastel background. Add cheerful text “Bye-Bye ” and a colorful kids-learning logo at the bottom. The toddler appears beside the hamster, waving happily and saying “Bye-bye!”

**Animation and camera:**

Smooth toddler walking and reaching movements, natural hand interaction with every door handle, realistic door-opening motion, expressive facial reactions, playful character animation, gentle camera movements, medium full-body framing, occasional slow push-ins, clean transitions between alphabet scenes, consistent character proportions, bright cinematic lighting, glossy 3D rendering, vibrant colors, soft shadows, highly detailed.

**Audio:**

Clear, sweet toddler voice saying each word once, timed with the on-screen text. Light, cheerful nursery-rhyme background music with soft sound effects (door creak, sparkle on reveal).
