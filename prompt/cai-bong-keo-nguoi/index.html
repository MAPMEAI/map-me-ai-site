Shadow Pulling

Nội dung prompt

REFERENCE IMAGE — IDENTITY LOCK

Use the woman in the reference image as the exact subject and primary identity source. The reference image determines the character's gender, facial identity, facial features, hairstyle, skin tone, and natural distinguishing characteristics. Preserve her identity with maximum accuracy. Do not replace her with a different person or invent a new character. The final image must be photorealistic.

SUBJECT AND POSE

The woman wears a plain black T-shirt, dark blue jeans, and red-and-white sneakers. Height: [.... cm]. Weight: [.... kg].

She is floating horizontally in midair, with her body tilted at approximately 35 degrees, as if being pulled by her own shadow. Both legs are raised toward the right, while her upper body leans toward the left. Her left arm hangs loosely downward, while her right hand grips the hem of her black T-shirt and pulls it toward the shadow on the wall.

Maintain realistic female body proportions consistent with the reference character. The floating pose must look physically convincing, with natural body positioning and believable clothing movement.

ENVIRONMENT AND BACKGROUND

The scene takes place in front of a minimalist gray concrete wall with natural surface textures, tiny pores, and visible vertical panel joints. A large, dark human silhouette stands upright on the wall, with one arm extended as if reaching out and pulling the woman toward itself.

The floor is made of gray concrete tiles. The shadow must be clearly visible and visually connected to the woman's pose, creating the illusion that her own shadow is pulling her into the air.

COMPOSITION AND PHOTOGRAPHY

Vertical full-body photography, 2:3 aspect ratio. The camera is positioned slightly in front of the subject, using a wide framing that captures her entire body, the complete shadow, and the surrounding environment.

Natural sunlight enters from the upper left, creating strong, sharply defined, realistic shadows on the wall and floor.

Creative streetwear editorial photography, realistic perspective, highly detailed fabric textures, natural skin texture, neutral color palette, crisp focus, professional camera quality, and convincing physical realism.

CRITICAL REQUIREMENTS

- Use the woman from the reference image as the actual subject, not as a face pasted onto a newly generated body.
- Preserve her original facial identity, hairstyle, skin tone, and distinguishing features.
- Keep the subject female, with realistic anatomy and natural body proportions.
- Maintain the specified floating pose and believable interaction between her right hand and the T-shirt.
- Ensure the shadow is large, dark, sharply defined, and positioned consistently with the lighting and scene.
- Keep the entire body visible, including both legs and both feet.
- Do not change the subject's identity or generate a different person.

NEGATIVE CONSTRAINTS

No male subject, no identity substitution, no pasted-on face, no distorted anatomy, no extra fingers, no duplicated limbs, no missing feet, no unnatural body proportions, no blurry shadow, no cartoon style, no plastic-looking skin, no artificial beauty filters, no text, no logos, and no watermarks.
