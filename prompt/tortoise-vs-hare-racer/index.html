Tortoise vs Hare Racer

Hoạt hình 3D Rùa và Thỏ đua chạy kèm Character Sheet đồng nhất.

Nội dung prompt

1. Character Sheet Prompt:
Professional 3D animation character sheet for two cartoon characters: a green turtle and a fluffy white rabbit. Pixar-style family animation, bright and cheerful. The sheet is split into two sections: top half for the turtle, bottom half for the rabbit. Each section includes: front view, side view, back view, and 3/4 view turnaround poses; a row of 4-5 expressions (neutral, smug, angry/grumpy, shocked, triumphant); and color swatches with hex codes. The turtle is bright green with a large cream-colored domed shell, short chubby limbs, round head, large glossy black eyes, small nostrils, and a small curved mouth. The rabbit is fluffy white all over with very long floppy ears showing pink inner ears, a small round pink nose, large glossy black eyes, short stubby limbs, and a small fluffy cotton tail. Clean white background, labeled with character names "Turbo the Turtle" and "Dash the Rabbit", model-sheet layout, highly detailed, consistent character design, studio-quality 3D render.

2. First Frame Image Prompt:
Vertical 9:16 movie-quality 3D Pixar-style cartoon render. At a black-and-white checkered starting line on a brown dirt race track, a cute green cartoon turtle with a light tan domed shell stands on the LEFT in a starting crouch, and a fluffy white rabbit with long ears stands on the RIGHT in a matching crouch, both wearing sly, mischievous half-smiles with eyes narrowed. A string of checkered pennant flags hangs above the track between two poles. Background: soft green grassy field under a clear bright blue sky. Bright even sunny daylight, saturated colors, clean and cheerful cartoon look, humorous fable atmosphere, high detail.

3. Image-to-Video Prompt:
Theme: A comedic twist on the Tortoise and the Hare — a slow turtle eats a super-spicy chili pepper, turns fiery red, rockets past the cocky rabbit, and wins the race.
Visuals: Brown dirt race track with checkered pennant banners, green grass and clear blue sky; a green turtle with a tan shell and a fluffy white rabbit; a small plant bearing one bright red chili pepper; the turtle glowing fiery red with a hot aura; rocket-exhaust flames; checkered finish line.
Camera: Smooth side-tracking shots following the race, comedic close-ups on the turtle’s face for the spicy reaction, fast chase motion during the rocket boost, triumphant final two-shot.
Style: Bright, colorful, high-quality 3D cartoon animation (Pixar-like), lighthearted and slapstick, exaggerated facial expressions and cartoon physics, vertical 9:16.
Action + Sound Design:
[0–10s] The race begins — the rabbit instantly dashes ahead while the turtle plods forward slowly; the rabbit runs cocky circles around the turtle and taunts him by waving a carrot; the turtle frowns in frustration — playful whimsical orchestral music, quick cartoon pitter-patter footsteps.
[10–14s] The turtle stops and spots a small plant with a single bright red chili pepper; he opens his mouth wide and swallows the pepper whole — brief silence, one crunchy bite sound.
[14–21s] Tight close-up: the turtle’s cheeks puff out, his eyes bulge, and his skin shifts from green to a glowing fiery red with a shimmering heat aura; he drops to all fours with a determined, mischievous grin — rising sizzle, low heat rumble building, dramatic comedic sting.
[21–26.5s] Flames erupt from behind the turtle like rocket exhaust; he blasts down the track in a fire trail and zooms past the shocked rabbit, whose jaw drops and ears flop as he fails to keep up — powerful rocket whoosh, rushing wind, cartoon gulp from the rabbit.
[26.5–30s] The turtle leaps across the checkered finish line; on landing he instantly turns back to green and the flames vanish; the rabbit tumbles dizzily across the line behind him; the turtle stands upright holding up a red chili pepper with a smug smile while the rabbit stares in disbelief — triumphant little ding, comedic tumble boings, rabbit’s stunned gasp; no spoken dialogue, only exaggerated SFX and a whimsical score.
