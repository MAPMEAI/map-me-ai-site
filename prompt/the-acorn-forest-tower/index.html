THE GLOWING ACORN TOWER | Google Flow | 10s | 9:16

Create an ultra-realistic macro DIY transformation video of tiny adult artisans converting a giant natural acorn on a mossy forest stump into a glowing miniature forest tower.

0–2s: Artisans arrive with tiny tools. One opens a section of the acorn shell like a door, revealing its hollow interior.

2–7s: In satisfying, physically believable stop-motion, they carve arched windows, install a spiral staircase, timber balconies, a twig entrance bridge, moss, ferns, and miniature golden window frames. Every piece is visibly carried and secured.

7–10s: An artisan switches on the top lantern. Warm champagne-gold light glows through the windows as the camera smoothly orbits the completed tower. Tiny artisans wave from a balcony and open the entrance door.

STYLE: Photorealistic macro photography, detailed acorn grain, real moss fibers, natural forest greens, warm golden illumination, shallow depth of field, cinematic lighting, premium commercial quality.

AUDIO: Woodland ambience, tiny footsteps, carving, hammer taps, and a lantern click. No dialogue, narration, subtitles, or music.

CONTINUITY: Same acorn, artisans, clothing, architecture, and forest setting throughout. Realistic scale, hand movements, object contact, and construction physics.

NEGATIVE: Cartoon, plastic textures, floating tools, instant magical construction, morphing architecture, distorted hands, extra limbs, flickering lights, excessive particles, smoke, fire, neon, shaky camera, text, watermarks, logos.
