Camera movement (single continuous take):
The camera begins high above Earth, descending smoothly through the atmosphere as city lights fade below. It glides past a private jet cutting through the clouds, then continues its descent, breaking through the cloud layer to reveal a vast desert at dawn. Without cutting, the camera swoops low across the dunes, flying alongside a flock of falcons gliding just above the sand. It smoothly follows a winding desert highway, chasing a sleek black sports car speeding beneath, sand trailing behind it. The camera rises fluidly as a futuristic skyline emerges from the desert horizon, then dives between the glass towers, weaving through the urban canyon as reflections ripple across the façades. It continues rising toward a rooftop, sweeping across an infinity pool with steam drifting through the air, as a flock of pigeons bursts into flight and scatters past the lens. The camera glides forward and settles into a slow push-in toward a young man in a tailored black suit standing at the rooftop’s edge, wind moving through his jacket, city sprawling behind him. The shot continues smoothly into a close-up as he adjusts his cufflink and turns his gaze toward the horizon, golden sunrise light flaring gently across his face, ending on a still, cinematic portrait with the skyline blurred softly behind him.
