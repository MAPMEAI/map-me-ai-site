Train Doorway Travel Photo

Ảnh du lịch ghép mặt vào toa tàu xanh trên cầu cạn, khung 4:5.

Miễn phíẢnhẢnh

Nội dung prompt

Create an ultra-realistic, cinematic travel photograph using two references: the uploaded personal photo for the person's identity, and the reference image for the exact scene, pose, composition, and atmosphere.

FACE & IDENTITY: Preserve the person's exact face, facial structure, skin tone, beard, eyes, and natural appearance from their personal photo. Do not copy the reference person's face. The final person must look exactly like the owner of the uploaded photo.

POSE & COMPOSITION: A stylish man standing at the open doorway of a moving blue passenger train, positioned along the far left side of the vertical frame. His arms are folded across his chest, one leg slightly crossed in front of the other, and he looks thoughtfully toward the distant mountains on the right. Show his full body, including both shoes, with a natural, relaxed posture.

OUTFIT: Black baseball cap, black short-sleeved T-shirt with a small minimalist white “PARIS” design and a short red-white accent, black cargo trousers, black-and-white high-top sneakers, and a black wristwatch. Keep the clothing realistic and consistent with the reference image.

SCENERY: A long blue-and-cream passenger train curves dramatically across a tall railway viaduct through lush green mountains. Below, a beautiful turquoise river flows between rocky banks and dense green forests. Distant layered mountain peaks rise beneath a vast blue sky filled with soft white clouds.

LIGHTING & MOOD: Warm golden-hour sunlight, natural highlights on the train, realistic shadows, atmospheric mountain haze, detailed foliage, sparkling river water, cinematic color grading, and a peaceful adventurous travel mood.

CAMERA & QUALITY: Vertical 4:5 composition, wide-angle travel photography, realistic depth, detailed train windows and metal surfaces, sharp facial features, natural skin texture, professional DSLR photography, high dynamic range, photorealistic 8K-style detail.

IMPORTANT DETAILS: Keep the person on the left and preserve the expansive mountain-and-river landscape on the right. Reproduce the reference image's perspective and composition as closely as possible. Maintain realistic body proportions, correct hands and feet, and natural train geometry. The person must be safely positioned within the doorway, with no impossible body angles.

Use the personal photo only for identity and the reference image for visual guidance. Do not add extra people, duplicate limbs, distorted shoes, watermarks, or unwanted text. The final result should look like a genuine professional travel photograph, not an illustration.
