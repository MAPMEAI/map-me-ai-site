Dog Washing Dishes

Chó lông xù rửa chén bằng chiếc bồn cầu ma thuật, hài hước.

Miễn phíVideoVideo

Nội dung prompt

First Frame Image Prompt:
A fluffy, light-brown Cockapoo-like dog standing upright on its hind legs in a modern bathroom, wearing a pink t-shirt, pink shorts, and a small baby bottle hanging from a strap across its chest. The dog’s paw is reaching out to pick up the top plate from a stack of dirty white plates covered in red tomato sauce, which sits on a small wooden side table next to the toilet. The toilet lid is raised and the bowl is empty. On the other side of the dog, a separate empty spot (such as a small shelf) is ready for clean plates. The bathroom has grey tiled walls, a white bathtub in the background, a small potted plant in a recessed wall niche, and soft, natural daylight. Whimsical, photorealistic, AI-video-ready composition, vertical frame.

Image-to-Video Prompt:
Theme: A surreal, humorous scene where an anthropomorphic fluffy dog “washes” dirty dishes in a glowing toilet, one plate at a time.
Visuals: A fluffy light-brown dog in a pink outfit with a tiny baby bottle strapped to its side. A small side table next to the toilet holds a stack of dirty, sauce-covered plates. A separate clean stack of white plates sits on a small shelf nearby. Modern grey-tiled bathroom with a white toilet and bathtub, and a magical glowing blue light inside the toilet bowl.
Camera: Static shot, fixed framing on the dog, the side table, and the toilet throughout [0-10s].
Action + Sound Design [0-10s]:
1. The dog picks up one dirty, sauce-covered plate from the top of the stack on the side table, leaving the rest in place. (Soft ceramic scrape)
2. The dog turns and drops the plate into the open toilet bowl. (Light ceramic clatter)
3. The dog closes the toilet lid and presses the flush button with its paw. (Toilet flush, brief water gurgle)
4. The dog stands with its arms crossed, waiting patiently. (Faint bathroom ambience)
5. The dog opens the lid to reveal a bright, glowing blue light from inside the bowl. (Sparkling shimmer)
6. The dog reaches in and pulls out one perfectly clean white plate. (Magical chime, water droplets)
7. The dog places the clean plate on the clean stack and looks satisfied. (Soft ceramic clink)
Style: Photorealistic, soft natural lighting, comedic and whimsical, everyday bathroom turned magical.
