PROMPT— FACEBOOK SCREEN BREAKOUT | 3D CINEMATIC MASTERPIECE

Aspect Ratio: 4:5 Vertical | Ultra-HD 8K | Hyper-Realistic 3D Render

Create a breathtaking, hyper-realistic cinematic 3D promotional scene using the uploaded reference image as the visual and character identity reference.

IDENTITY LOCK:
Use the exact person from the reference image as the main character. Preserve their facial features, hairstyle, skin tone, outfit and accessories exactly as shown in the reference image. Do not replace them with a random person.

MAIN SCENE:
A gigantic premium smartphone stands vertically on a glossy reflective marble floor. Its screen displays a realistic Facebook interface featuring the [PROFILE NAME] profile.

The main character is dramatically breaking through the smartphone screen, with his upper body emerging through a spectacular shattered-glass opening. His right arm reaches outside the screen, grabbing the hand or shirt of a tiny miniature version of himself sitting on the floor.

The miniature character has the same recognizable face and hairstyle, wearing the same outfit as in the reference image, with a small golden [LOGO] emblem on the shirt. They look hilariously shocked and terrified, with exaggerated natural body language.

FACEBOOK SCREEN DETAILS:

- Facebook blue branding and interface.
- Black-and-gold circular [LOGO] crown logo.
- [PROFILE NAME]
- [@USERNAME]
- [NUMBER] followers
- [YOUR TAGLINE]
- Engagement reactions and counts.
- Like, Comment and Share buttons.
- Facebook bottom navigation icons.

CINEMATIC EFFECTS:

- Thousands of realistic blue-tinted glass fragments exploding outward.
- Electric-blue glowing fracture lines.
- Realistic depth, shadows and reflections.
- Premium metallic smartphone frame.
- Beautiful soft studio background with subtle greenery.
- Ultra-detailed skin, hair, clothing and realistic miniature proportions.

COMPOSITION:
Keep the giant smartphone on the left and the miniature character on the lower right. The main character must emerge naturally from the screen, with believable physical interaction. Every object should feel naturally integrated into the environment, never artificially inserted.

VISUAL QUALITY:
Photorealistic 3D CGI, premium commercial advertising aesthetic, cinematic studio lighting, realistic materials, sharp focus, physically accurate reflections, extraordinary micro-details, professional composition.

STRICT REQUIREMENTS:
No random characters, no altered identity, no extra fingers, no distorted faces, no floating objects without physical logic, no unnecessary decorations, no watermark.

FINAL RESULT: A stunning, scroll-stopping [PROFILE NAME] Facebook promotional image that looks like a premium 3D advertising campaign.
