Nội dung prompt

A professional digital art collage featuring a handsome young Asian man with styled curly black hair and a warm smile. The central full-body figure of the man stands on a reflective wet rooftop at night, wearing a black zip-up athletic jacket, black cargo pants, and a backpack, looking slightly to his left. Overlapping in the background is a large, translucent close-up portrait of the same man smiling directly at the camera. A magnificent, vibrant blue and green peacock with detailed iridescent feathers stands majestically on the left side of the composition. In the background, a glowing city skyline at dusk with lit skyscrapers is reflected on the water below, under a dramatic dark twilight sky with orange and blue hues. The overall composition is a high-contrast edit with double exposure effect and cinematic lighting.
