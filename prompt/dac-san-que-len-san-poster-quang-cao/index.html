# CINEMATIC FOOD ADVERTISING POSTER — MẬP MÊ AI

Create a premium, photorealistic cinematic advertising poster for the Vietnamese dried snakehead fish brand **“MẬP MÊ AI”**, featuring the product **“KHÔ CÁ LÓC”**. Vertical 2:3 composition, high-detail commercial food photography, luxurious rustic Vietnamese Mekong Delta atmosphere, warm golden-hour lighting.

**BRAND IDENTITY AND TYPOGRAPHY — ESSENTIAL:**

Display the exact brand name **“MẬP MÊ AI”** prominently in the upper-left area of the poster. Use large, bold, handcrafted Vietnamese-inspired lettering in deep roasted-brown, with clean, perfectly legible characters and all Vietnamese diacritics rendered correctly. Create a distinctive original fish-and-river-wave emblem above or beside the wordmark. The brand name must be a major visual element, clearly readable at mobile-screen size.

Display the exact product name **“KHÔ CÁ LÓC”** in a separate, prominent headline beneath the brand name or in the lower-middle area. Use bold, elegant typography in warm ivory or golden cream with subtle dark-brown contrast. Preserve the exact Vietnamese spelling and diacritics. The product name must be clearly readable and visually distinct from the brand name.

**HERO PRODUCT:**

In the central foreground, showcase a generous arrangement of authentic-looking dried snakehead fish on a round woven bamboo tray lined with fresh green banana leaves. The fish should have realistic elongated shapes, natural golden-brown and dark caramel tones, visible fibrous texture, slightly curled edges, and subtle highlights that reveal the dried surface. Make the fish look appetizing and authentic, never plastic, artificial, or excessively oily.

Behind the tray, position a premium transparent food package containing dried snakehead fish, with a kraft-paper label displaying the exact brand name **“MẬP MÊ AI”** and product name **“KHÔ CÁ LÓC”**. The package should look professionally designed, realistic, upright, and clearly visible. Use the same brand identity and typography as the main poster. This is a conceptual package design, not a claim that it matches an existing retail package.

**FOOD STYLING:**

In the lower-right foreground, include a ceramic plate of grilled snakehead fish with lightly charred edges, fresh red chili, herbs, and realistic cooked texture. Add a small blue-and-white ceramic bowl of chili dipping sauce, fresh cucumber slices, leafy greens, chopsticks, and a bowl of steamed white rice. Keep these supporting elements balanced and subordinate to the hero product.

**ENVIRONMENT AND STORYTELLING:**

Set the scene in a rustic riverside kitchen in Vietnam's Mekong Delta at sunset. Include a charcoal grill with glowing embers on the left, delicate cooking smoke drifting through the warm light, a softly blurred wooden boat on a calm river, tropical greenery, and subtle woven bamboo textures. The background should suggest traditional Vietnamese food culture without distracting from the product.

**LIGHTING AND CAMERA:**

Use dramatic golden-hour backlighting, warm cinematic rim light, soft volumetric rays, natural atmospheric haze, realistic shadows, rich amber highlights, deep but detailed brown tones, and a sophisticated warm-cool color balance. Capture the scene from a slightly elevated three-quarter angle with a commercial food-photography lens, shallow depth of field, sharp product details, and soft background bokeh. Maintain clear separation between the foreground fish, the package, and the riverside background.

**COMPOSITION:**

Establish a strong visual hierarchy: first the brand name “MẬP MÊ AI”, second the dried snakehead fish hero product, third the product headline “KHÔ CÁ LÓC”. Reserve clean negative space in the upper-left for typography. Keep all essential text away from the edges. Ensure the package, fish tray, and cooked serving remain visually distinct and recognizable. The final image must look like a professionally art-directed food campaign, not a generic food photograph.

**TEXT ACCURACY:**

The only required prominent text is:
“MẬP MÊ AI”
“KHÔ CÁ LÓC”

Render both strings exactly as written, with correct Vietnamese accents, consistent spelling, clean letterforms, and no duplicated characters. Do not substitute, translate, misspell, or distort either name. Do not invent promotional claims, prices, certifications, or additional slogans.

**QUALITY AND NEGATIVE CONSTRAINTS:**

Photorealistic food textures, premium commercial finish, high detail, natural materials, refined color grading, professional advertising composition. No cartoon style, no illustration, no fake brand names, no incorrect Vietnamese typography, no random letters, no excessive smoke, no oversized flames, no deformed fish, no plastic-looking food, no cluttered layout, no watermark, no irrelevant props, no low-resolution details.

Final output: one complete, premium vertical 2:3 advertising poster for **MẬP MÊ AI — KHÔ CÁ LÓC**, with the brand name and product name visibly integrated into the artwork.
