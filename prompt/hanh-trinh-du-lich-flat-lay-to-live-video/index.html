Hành Trình Du Lịch (Flat-lay to Live Video)

Combo 4 kịch bản biến ảnh chụp đồ du lịch Flat-lay thành video chuyển động.

Miễn phíVideoVideo

Nội dung prompt

First Frame Image Prompt:
A flat-lay travel photography scene featuring a vintage brown leather travel journal titled “JOURNAL DE VOYAGE” in elegant gold serif lettering, with a small compass rose emblem below the title. The journal is secured with a leather strap and brass buckle, and a slim brass pen is tucked underneath the strap. The journal sits on top of a folded vintage map showing Italy and the Mediterranean coastline. Surrounding the journal are travel accessories: a pair of tortoiseshell sunglasses at the top left, black over-ear headphones with a coiled cable at the top right, a small vintage brass compass near the bottom edge, and two polaroid-style photographs — one showing the Roman Colosseum and another showing a woman standing on a sunlit European street. Bright, clean natural daylight, soft shadows, light-colored wooden or marble surface, wanderlust aesthetic, highly detailed photorealistic style, portrait orientation.

Video Prompt 1 (Stop-Motion Entry):
Turn this image into a stop motion video, with the various items entering the frame one by one from the edge they’re closest to.

Video Prompt 2 (Stepping Into Polaroid):
Hold on the full flat-lay for a beat, then begin a slow, smooth push-in (dolly zoom) toward the polaroid of the woman standing on the sunlit European street. As the camera moves closer, the edges of the polaroid softly blur and dissolve into the frame — the photo’s borders melt away and the still image seamlessly transitions into live-action motion: the woman turns her head slightly, her hair catches the breeze, sunlight flickers through nearby leaves, and pedestrians blur past in the background of the cobblestone street. End the video by zooming out to the original flat-lay.

Video Prompt 3 (Magical Unboxing Journal):
Animate the leather strap unbuckling on its own and sliding off. The journal cover lifts open as if by an invisible hand, revealing blank cream-colored pages. As the pages turn, travel items begin emerging in stop-motion fashion from between them — first the tortoiseshell sunglasses sliding out to the top left, then the black over-ear headphones unfurling to the top right, followed by the brass compass popping up near the bottom edge. Finally, two polaroid photographs slide out from the back pages and land in place.

Video Prompt 4 (Invisible Hand Writing):
A brass pen enters from the right and begins to move across the open blank page as if guided by an invisible hand. Elegant cursive handwriting appears in dark ink stroke by stroke, spelling out a short travel journal entry (“Rome, day one — the light here is unlike anywhere else...”). As the writing continues, a small sketch of the Colosseum silhouette forms in the corner of the page. Once complete, the pen gently lowers and comes to rest diagonally across the page.
