Style: Warm, cozy, photorealistic editorial food photography with playful magical-realism; seamless 2D-to-3D pop-out effect; vertical 9:16 social format; rich creams, browns, ambers and gold tones; smooth flowing rhythm

The printed chocolate truffle cake photo morphs into a real glossy 3D cake that pops straight up out of the page, hovering and slightly rotating as chocolate flakes scatter — soft magical pop + gentle paper crinkle

The camera continues flowing over a text-heavy menu page where a chocolate chip cookie emerges from the list and floats above the words — delicate pop

The scroll arrives at "COOKIES & DOUGHNUTS"; a grid of cookies springs up and a large chocolate doughnut with colorful sprinkles rises majestically from the bottom of the page, chocolate shavings falling — bouncy joyful pop
