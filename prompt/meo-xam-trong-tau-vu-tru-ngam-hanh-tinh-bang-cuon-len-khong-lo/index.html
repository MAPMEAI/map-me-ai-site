Nội dung prompt

Photorealistic cinematic scene inside a near-future interplanetary spacecraft, rendered with extreme realism and intricate physical detail. A calm domestic short-haired gray cat sits beside a large oval observation window, its posture upright and attentive, expression quietly curious. One paw is gently raised against the glass as the cat peers outward into deep space with focused fascination. Far beyond the spacecraft, suspended against the immense black void, floats an unimaginably gigantic planet-sized ball of yarn. The colossal yarn world is now much farther away, allowing its overwhelming scale to become fully visible against the surrounding cosmos. Its curved horizon dominates a vast section of space, partially illuminated by distant starlight, with enormous swirling layers of tightly wound yarn wrapping around the sphere like continental formations. Thick braided strands hundreds of kilometers wide create visible ridges and valleys across its surface, while loose fibers and tangled thread clouds drift around it like orbital debris. Tiny nearby moons, spacecraft silhouettes, and scattered asteroid fields emphasize the impossible planetary scale of the yarn sphere. The surrounding space feels immense and authentic: dense starfields stretch endlessly into darkness, faint nebula haze glows in the far distance, and subtle cosmic dust catches the light around the massive yarn planet. The scale contrast between the small spacecraft interior and the enormous distant object creates a strong sense of awe and isolation. The spacecraft interior feels believable and engineered rather than fantastical: brushed titanium surfaces, matte white composite panels, embedded touch displays, recessed ambient lighting, subtle wear marks, exposed fasteners, thermal insulation textures, and delicate reflections on curved glass. The oval window is thick multi-layered transparent material with slight edge distortion and microscopic scratches visible in the light. Cool instrument illumination softly lights the cabin while faint reflected light from the distant yarn planet subtly highlights the cat's fur with extraordinary detail — individual hairs sharply visible, including nuanced textures around the ears, whiskers, and the raised paw pressed lightly to the glass. Reflections of navigation holograms faintly appear in the window without distracting from the exterior view. The atmosphere is quiet, lonely, contemplative, and subtly surreal, evoking wonder and introspection during deep-space travel. Ultra-detailed, cinematic depth of field, physically accurate lighting, high dynamic range, realistic shadows, natural color grading, premium science-fiction realism
