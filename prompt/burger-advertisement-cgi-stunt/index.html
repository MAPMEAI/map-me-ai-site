Burger Advertisement (CGI Stunt)

Quảng cáo burger 8s, bay qua đường đua 3D ban đêm

Miễn phíVideoẨm thực

Nội dung prompt

burger advertisement
Create an approximately 8-second vertical 9:16 cinematic CGI burger advertisement, closely following the attached reference video's action, camera movement, lighting and timing. Recreate the advertisement itself, excluding all Instagram interface elements, phone status bars, captions, buttons and usernames.
VISUAL STYLE:
Photorealistic food rendered within a dramatic 3D stunt arena at night. Nearly black background, towering gray metal half-pipe ramp, bright red neon strips along both edges, a white centerline, visible steel support structures and distant white stadium floodlights. Glossy surfaces, realistic shadows and cinematic motion blur.
HERO PRODUCT:
One realistic cheeseburger with a golden-brown glossy bun, thick grilled beef patty, melted yellow cheese, green lettuce, red tomato and sauce. Maintain the same burger and ingredients throughout. No face, arms, legs or wheels.
ACTION AND CAMERA:
0–2 seconds:
Start with a wide view of the enormous curved ramp. A small burger accelerates across the bottom and rapidly travels up the steep ramp. The camera follows closely from behind and slightly above, tilting upward as the burger reaches the lip. Red neon edges create strong leading lines.
2–3.5 seconds:
The burger launches high above the ramp into the black night sky. Follow it upward as the ramp falls out of frame. The burger tumbles slowly end over end, briefly revealing its top bun, side layers and underside.
3.5–5 seconds:
Two small, brilliant white rocket-like exhaust jets flare from opposite sides of the burger. The jets rotate with it during its aerial tumble, leaving smoke trails. Keep the burger intact and recognizable.
5–6 seconds:
The jets extinguish into two thick white smoke trails. The camera follows the burger descending rapidly toward the arena floor. Rotate it back into an upright serving position before landing.
6–8 seconds:
The burger lands at the center of a dark, wet, reflective platform. A dense white cloud of smoke bursts outward along the ground and billows behind it. Transition smoothly into a low-angle close-up hero shot. The burger fills the lower center of the frame, sharply detailed and warmly lit. Stadium lights glow through the smoke; the red-edged ramp remains visible on the right. Finish with a subtle camera push toward the burger.
At the final reveal, place a large white vintage-style “MapMeAI Create Burger” wordmark above the burger, matching the reference's placement.
Keep the action fluid and continuous. Preserve realistic food textures and consistent burger proportions. Avoid extra burgers, detached ingredients, cartoon styling, people, unrelated objects and additional text.
