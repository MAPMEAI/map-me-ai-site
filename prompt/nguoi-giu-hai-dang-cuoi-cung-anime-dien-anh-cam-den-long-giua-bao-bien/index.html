Nội dung prompt

Concept & Archetype: An adult woman, the final keeper of a lighthouse being decommissioned tomorrow. Unspoken Story: She has kept the light for decades for ships that stopped coming long ago. Shot & Composition: Dramatic low-angle shot from the cliff grass, the lighthouse towering behind her, her figure at the cliff's edge facing the sea. Pose & Gesture: Standing with her back half-turned to the viewer, one hand holding an old brass lantern at her side, the other clutching her coat closed at the collar. Expression & Face: Seen in three-quarter profile, calm and tired, eyes fixed on the horizon, a small tear line catching light. Hair & Wind: Long dark auburn hair loosely braided, the braid and loose strands streaming wildly in a sea gale. Costume & Silhouette: Heavy charcoal oilskin coat with a fringed knitted shawl over the shoulders flapping behind her, long wool skirt, worn boots. Keepsake: The brass lantern, its small flame flickering against the wind. Environment: A wind-scoured clifftop covered in sea thrift and white wildflowers, a white-and-faded-red lighthouse, a restless grey-green sea far below. Sky & Clouds: Storm clouds breaking at dusk, heavy painterly slate-grey masses edged with pink and apricot light, patches of pale teal sky. Lighting: Last light of dusk behind the clouds, the lighthouse beam slicing across the sky, warm lantern glow on her hands and face. Camera & Lens: 35mm, f/2.8, focus on her face and lantern, foreground flowers blurred. Atmosphere & Particles: Sea spray, petals torn loose and flying, light rain beginning. Color Palette: Slate grey, faded red, apricot, sea green, warm lantern amber. Style & Render Tags: Melancholic painterly anime illustration, expressive brush texture, dramatic sky, cinematic wind, solitude, masterpiece.
