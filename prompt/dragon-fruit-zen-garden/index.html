Dragon Fruit Zen Garden

Ảnh macro vườn Zen Nhật thu nhỏ trong nửa quả thanh long, khung 4:5.

Miễn phíẢnhẢnh

Nội dung prompt

Aspect Ratio: 4:5 vertical | Ultra-high resolution | Photorealistic cinematic macro photography.

MAIN CONCEPT:

Create an extraordinary miniature Japanese Zen garden built inside a giant, freshly sliced dragon fruit, displayed on a polished dark walnut wooden pedestal against a clean, pastel sky-blue studio background. The composition should resemble a luxury handcrafted miniature diorama photographed with professional macro photography.

HERO STRUCTURE — DRAGON FRUIT:

A large, naturally curved half of a vibrant magenta-pink dragon fruit forms the entire base of the miniature world. Its thick pink rind has realistic organic texture, subtle imperfections, and distinctive green-tipped scales. The exposed inner flesh creates a curved white border dotted with tiny, sharply defined black seeds. The fruit must look fresh, natural, and physically believable, with no artificial plastic appearance.

MINIATURE JAPANESE TEA HOUSE:

A beautifully crafted traditional Japanese wooden tea house sits toward the rear-right of the fruit. Include a layered dark-brown thatched roof, timber beams, authentic shoji-style grid windows, a raised wooden veranda, tiny railings, and short wooden entrance steps. Warm amber lanterns illuminate the interior, revealing a cozy tea room with a low table, cushions, and subtle Japanese-inspired decor.

MINIATURE LANDSCAPE:

Fill the fruit interior with an intricately designed Japanese garden featuring lush green moss, miniature bamboo plants, carefully arranged natural stones, tiny shrubs, and a winding stone pathway. A delicate multi-tier waterfall cascades over dark rocks into a crystal-clear miniature pond near the front-right. Include realistic water reflections, tiny ripples, transparent shallow water, and detailed wet stone textures.

CHERRY BLOSSOM TREE:

On the rear-left, place a miniature bonsai-inspired cherry blossom tree with twisting dark branches and abundant soft-pink flowers. The delicate blossoms contrast beautifully with the vivid dragon fruit and surrounding green foliage.

HUMAN SUBJECT:

A photorealistic miniature-scale young adult man with dark styled hair, a neatly trimmed beard, a plain white T-shirt, black trousers, and white sneakers sits naturally on a tiny wooden stool near the left-center. He gently pours tea from a small dark ceramic teapot into a teacup on a low round wooden table. Preserve realistic anatomy, natural hands, accurate proportions, and convincing contact with the miniature furniture.

COMPOSITION & CAMERA:

Front-facing three-quarter macro view, slightly elevated camera angle, entire dragon fruit and wooden pedestal visible. The fruit dominates the composition, with the tea house, cherry blossom tree, human figure, and waterfall all clearly distinguishable. Balanced visual hierarchy, rich foreground detail, layered depth, realistic miniature scale, and precise craftsmanship.

LIGHTING & COLOR:

Soft daylight from the upper-left, warm golden lantern illumination, gentle ambient shadows, natural specular highlights on water, vibrant magenta-pink fruit, fresh emerald greenery, warm walnut wood, delicate pink blossoms, and a clean powder-blue background.

RENDERING STYLE:

Ultra-photorealistic macro photography, luxury miniature diorama, museum-quality craftsmanship, physically based materials, realistic global illumination, ray-traced reflections, exceptional micro-textures, cinematic depth of field, crisp hero details, natural color grading, professional product-photography composition.

NEGATIVE PROMPT:

Cartoon, anime, illustration, toy-like plastic materials, low resolution, blurry details, distorted anatomy, extra fingers, malformed hands, duplicate people, oversized furniture, floating objects, unrealistic waterfall, muddy water, artificial flowers, oversaturated colors, excessive bloom, flat lighting, cluttered composition, text, watermark, logos, cropped pedestal, deformed dragon fruit, inconsistent shadows, unrealistic proportions
