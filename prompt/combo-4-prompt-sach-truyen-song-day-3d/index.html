Nội dung prompt

1. Wind-Up Robot

First Frame Image Prompt:
A vintage children's picture book lies open on a wooden table, showing a charming illustration of a retro tin toy robot with a rectangular body, antenna and round eyes, standing on a cartoon toy-shop floor. A large wind-up key is visible on its back. A realistic human hand enters from above, fingers gripping the illustrated key. Close-up overhead angle, soft warm window light, nostalgic playful atmosphere, photorealistic style, 4K.

Image-to-Video Prompt:
The hand twists the key in slow, clicking turns. The robot's illustrated body shudders, then lifts away from the page as a three-dimensional tin toy with a metallic, slightly scuffed sheen. It takes stiff, jerky steps forward across the open book, arms swinging and antenna wobbling. The hand withdraws as the robot marches. The painted floor stays flat beneath its feet. Camera tracks sideways alongside the robot, smooth motion, 6 seconds.

2. Genie Lamp Rub

First Frame Image Prompt:
A cozy children's picture book lies open on a wooden table, showing a detailed illustration of a golden oil lamp with a curved spout, sitting on a cartoon desert rock at dusk. A realistic human index finger and thumb enter from the right, with the fingertip resting against the illustrated lamp's side as if about to rub it. Close-up shot, dim purple twilight with a warm golden glow, magical atmosphere, photorealistic style, 4K.

Image-to-Video Prompt:
The fingertip rubs the illustrated lamp in slow circles. Shimmering blue-violet smoke curls up from the spout in three dimensions, swirling above the page and glowing softly. The smoke thickens and forms a friendly genie with a wide smile and folded arms, floating above the open book with a tail of smoke where his legs should be. Sparkles drift down onto the pages, and the lamp stays flat on the page. Camera pushes in slowly, then eases up to reveal the genie's face, smooth motion, 6 seconds.

3. Pop-Up Castle

First Frame Image Prompt:
A vintage children's picture book lies open on a wooden table, showing a detailed illustration of a fairy-tale castle gate with a wooden drawbridge, stone walls and tiny flags, drawn flat in storybook style. A realistic human hand enters from the left, fingertips pressing the edge of the illustrated gate as if about to open it. Close-up shot from a low angle, soft morning light, enchanted fairy-tale atmosphere, photorealistic style, 4K.

Image-to-Video Prompt:
The hand pushes the illustrated gate open. The page unfolds like a pop-up book, and stone towers, turrets and walls rise upward in three dimensions with a paper-and-stone texture. The drawbridge lowers onto the page, and tiny flags unfurl and flutter on the tower tops. Warm light glows from the castle windows. The hand withdraws as the castle settles into place. Camera cranes upward slowly to reveal the full castle, smooth motion, 6 seconds.

4. Dragon Egg Hatch

First Frame Image Prompt:
A whimsical children's picture book lies open on a wooden table, showing a detailed illustration of a speckled green-and-purple dragon egg resting in a nest of straw and twigs inside a cartoon cave. A realistic human hand enters from above, one finger extended and about to tap the illustrated egg. Extreme close-up, macro angle, warm glowing cave light, magical atmosphere, photorealistic style, 4K.

Image-to-Video Prompt:
The fingertip taps the illustrated egg twice. Cracks spread across its surface, and the shell breaks away in three-dimensional pieces that drop onto the page. A baby dragon with shiny scales and big curious eyes climbs out, shakes itself and blinks at the hand. It opens its mouth and lets out a tiny puff of real flame and smoke. The nest and cave stay flat on the page. Camera slowly pushes in on the dragon's face, smooth motion, 6 seconds.
