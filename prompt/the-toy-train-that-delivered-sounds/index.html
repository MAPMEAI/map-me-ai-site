The Toy Train That Delivered Sounds

Video 3D 10s tàu đồ chơi chở thú con đến chơi cùng bé 2 tuổi, vui nhộn.

Miễn phíVideoVideo

Nội dung prompt

Create a 10-second, 9:16 vertical premium colorful 3D animated video. Maintain one adorable 2-year-old toddler boy with round cheeks, brown eyes, soft dark hair, and a bright blue T-shirt.

**0–2s:** A colorful toy train races around a playroom track. Three carriage roofs pop open simultaneously, revealing a baby lion, tiny elephant, and fluffy duckling. The toddler drops to his knees in amazement.

**2–5s:** The train stops beside him. The lion cub roars softly, the elephant trumpets, and the duck quacks. The toddler points and says, “Lion! Elephant! Duck!”

**5–8s:** The animals hop safely onto a soft play mat and make their sounds in a playful rhythm. The toddler claps along as the train wheels spin gently.

**8–10s:** The train gives a cheerful whistle. All three animals perform a perfectly timed sound chorus; the toddler laughs and the train headlight flashes like a tiny spotlight.

Smooth tracking shot, clear animal-to-sound synchronization, recognizable sounds, cute expressions, polished 3D textures, vibrant colors, gentle rhythmic music. No mismatched sounds, distorted animals, flickering, chaotic movement, abrupt cuts, subtitles, logos, or watermarks.
