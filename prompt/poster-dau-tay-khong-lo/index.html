Poster Dâu Tây Khổng Lồ

Poster thông tin dâu tây khổng lồ phong cách miniature: người tí hon leo núi trên quả dâu, nhãn STRAWBERRY / SWEET SEEDS. Bản tách từ Master Prompt Poster Rau Củ.

Miễn phíẢnhẨm thực

Nội dung prompt

Create a photographic information poster introducing strawberry as one spring fruit. Use a monumental megalithic scenarization: the produce’s main form fills both sides and the depth of the frame at close range, with bold cropping that creates a foreground porch, layered background, and traversable natural space. Show a giant strawberry in a low-angle view, its seed-studded red surface like a cliff face, with the green calyx leaves arching overhead as a canopy and a second berry softly blurred in the background. Preserve crisp material texture, edge thickness, tiny water droplets, translucent highlights, and side or back light passing through the produce. Add tiny scale cues derived from strawberry near the bottom, such as miniature climbers with ropes on the red slope and a tiny picker with a basket, with a shallow water or highly reflective surface that subtly mirrors the produce, scale cues, and shadows. Keep the background dark, clean, and softly layered, separate foreground and background with shallow depth of field, and extract bright controlled colors from the produce’s own material. Use a large area of clean strawberry red, dark structure shadows, and small high-brightness accents at scale cues or information nodes. Add concise high-contrast information typography about strawberry in a clean font with ample whitespace, placed in the upper right, slightly separated from the main form to create a three-layer reading path: photographic subject, miniature scale, and typographic information. Make this one distinct slide in a ten-slide spring produce series, varying layout from the other slides while retaining a clear knowledge logic and a memorable golden-sentence message. Keep the image bright, clear, moist, lively, and free of dusty, old, or smoky treatment. Use exactly two short factual labels: “STRAWBERRY” and “SWEET SEEDS”. Keep typography fully inside the frame with comfortable margins. All miniature props are original and unbranded.
