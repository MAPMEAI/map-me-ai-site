Dumpling City

Thành phố bánh bao tí hon sống động với dòng sông sa tế.

Miễn phíVideoẨm thực

Nội dung prompt

First Frame Image Prompt:
A whimsical surreal miniature cityscape aerial view, entire buildings shaped like giant soup dumplings xiaolongbao and fluffy steamed baozi with pleated dough textures and soft bun surfaces, a winding river of deep reddish-orange chili oil and spicy broth flowing through the center of the city, whole red chili peppers floating in the river like boats, arched bridges connecting dumpling buildings, tiny pedestrians walking along riverbanks and crossing bridges, European-style domes and tiled roofs blended with dumpling architecture, warm golden natural sunlight casting soft shadows, tilt-shift miniature diorama effect, highly detailed 3D render, stop-motion animation aesthetic, cozy appetizing color palette, food art photography, cinematic aerial establishing shot, 9:16 vertical composition.

Image-to-Video Prompt:
Theme: Whimsical miniature metropolis built entirely from dumplings, steamed buns, and chili oil.
Visuals: Giant xiaolongbao and baozi form domed buildings and skyscrapers; a deep reddish-orange river of chili oil winds through the city with whole red chili peppers floating like boats; streets paved with black sesame seeds; tiny chefs, cyclists, construction workers, and pedestrians interact with the edible architecture; woven dough bridges, dumpling fountains, chili-ring stools, and a sesame-covered dough construction site.
Camera: Aerial tilt-shift establishing shot slowly pushing in, then low street-level tracking shots, gentle dollies past construction and dining areas, final wide aerial pull-back revealing distant dumpling mountains.
Style: Cinematic stop-motion miniature diorama, warm golden sunlight, soft shadows, highly detailed 3D food art, playful surreal appetizing color palette, vertical 9:16 format.
Action + Sound Design:
[0-1.5s] Aerial view of the dumpling city reveals a winding chili-oil river with red chili peppers drifting like boats — gentle traditional Chinese flute and plucked strings begin.
[1.5-3.5s] Street-level scene: a chef, cyclist, and pedestrians move along a road paved with black sesame seeds, passing buildings with dumpling roofs — soft footsteps, bicycle bell, sizzle of steam.
[3.5-5.5s] A modern avenue with skyscrapers stacked from dumplings; a delivery cart shaped like a giant dumpling on wheels waits at a crosswalk — city ambience, distant traffic hum, cart wheels creaking.
[5.5-7.5s] Construction workers in yellow hard hats assemble a sesame-covered dough building while nearby figures sip tea at tables with chili-ring stools — hammer taps, clinking teacups, casual chatter.
[7.5-10s] Wide view of a woven dough bridge over the chili-oil river, a dumpling fountain spraying water, then camera pulls back to show dumpling pagodas and mountains in the distance — flowing water, fountain splash, music swells gently.
